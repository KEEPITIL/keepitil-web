PokaSnap

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026 · PokaSnap is made by KEEPITIL.

The short version: your photos stay on your device. PokaSnap never uploads your camera images. You can play as a guest without an account, and you can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

Camera

PokaSnap uses your camera so you can take photos with your pet. The live camera image is processed only on your device to show your pet on screen and to create the photo you take. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Your photos

Photos you take are stored in the "My Snaps" album inside the app, on your device only. A photo leaves your device only when you choose to — by tapping Save (which saves it to your phone's photo library) or Share (which opens your device's share sheet). PokaSnap asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you save, and it never reads your existing photos. If you choose "Use a photo" instead of the camera, the photo you pick is used on your device only.

Accounts

You can play as a guest; guest progress is stored only on your device. If you choose to create an account (with email, Apple or Google), we store your email address and a unique account ID so you can sign in. PokaSnap accounts are KEEPITIL accounts, stored with our provider Supabase. If you sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email.

What we don't collect

  • No location.
  • No microphone or audio.
  • No contacts.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising.
  • No selling of personal data, ever.

Gameplay events

The app records simple gameplay events (for example "photo taken" or "mission completed") on your device to help the game work. These events contain no photos, names or free text. This version of PokaSnap does not send them to any server.

Purchases

This version of PokaSnap has no purchases. Items are unlocked by playing and with coins earned in the game.

Children

PokaSnap is designed to be friendly for all ages and contains no chat, social feeds, messaging or public profiles. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; a guest can play without providing any.

Deleting your data

Account: in the app go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your KEEPITIL account and associated data. On your device: Settings → Start over erases your pet, progress and snaps; deleting the app removes them too. You can also email info@keepitil.com to request deletion.

Contact

Questions? Email info@keepitil.com.
