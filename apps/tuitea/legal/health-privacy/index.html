Health & Apple Health

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Health information is treated differently from everything else in TUITEA. This page says exactly how, because a general privacy policy is the wrong place to bury it.

What TUITEA reads, and only if you allow it

TUITEA can read steps, workouts, weight, heart rate, sleep and similar readings from Apple Health. Apple asks your permission for each kind, you grant it, and you can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings or by turning Apple Health off inside TUITEA. Turning it off in TUITEA clears the Apple-sourced values it was holding.

You can also type health entries in yourself without connecting Apple Health at all.

Where it goes

Health information leaves your device only when a TUITEA or Tupu feature you have explicitly enabled requires it, and then only the minimum needed for that feature. If you turn health integration off, that transmission stops.

In practice, today, health questions are answered on your device or from your own records: Tupu reads your latest weight, your recent sleep, a measurement you logged, and answers directly. No remote AI provider is approved to receive health information, so none receives it. Any external AI processing of sensitive health information would happen only where the applicable privacy and provider requirements are satisfied.

What never happens

  • TUITEA does not sell health data. Not to anyone, for any price.
  • TUITEA does not use health data for advertising, and does not share it with advertisers.
  • TUITEA does not send health data to a model because it might be useful. Transmission has to be required by a feature you switched on.

Who else can see it

Your health records are yours. Sharing them with your household is a choice you make per record, not a default.

Removing it

Delete individual entries in the app, turn Apple Health off to stop new readings arriving, or delete your account to remove your records entirely. Account deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
