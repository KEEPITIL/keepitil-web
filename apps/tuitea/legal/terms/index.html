Family plans

Free costs nothing and has no time limit: one signed-in account, the family tree with unlimited generations, memories and family records kept on your device, export and account deletion. Tupu (TUITEA's AI assistant) is not included in Free.

TUITEA Plus — $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year — adds Tupu and lets up to seven signed-in accounts in total (including yours) share the household. Tupu's use is subject to a monthly allowance for each person and for the household; when it is reached, Tupu says so and resumes the next month, and everything else keeps working. Tupu reads answers aloud with your device's built-in voice; live conversational AI voice is not offered.

TUITEA Pro is coming soon and cannot be purchased yet.

The seven accounts are TUITEA household accounts, not Apple Family Sharing. If a Plus plan ends, nobody is removed and nothing is deleted: Tupu pauses, and new members cannot join beyond what Free allows, until a plan is restarted.

Family Storage subscriptions

By default, photos, videos and documents are kept on your own device. Optional Family Storage plans keep copies in TUITEA's online storage, shared by the whole household, separately from Free or Plus:

  • 8 GB — $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year
  • 25 GB — $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year
  • 80 GB — $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year
  • 250 GB — $29.99 a month or $299.99 a year

Households created from 14 October 2026 have no online storage without a Family Storage plan. Households created before that date keep the 1 GB of online storage they already had at no cost.

Prices are in US dollars; the App Store shows the price for your country before you confirm. A plan belongs to the household, not to one phone: everyone in the household shares it.

When a paid storage plan ends. If a plan is cancelled, expires, is refunded or cannot be renewed, the household returns to its free online-storage allowance (none for households created from 14 October 2026; 1 GB for earlier households) once the paid period is over. Nothing you have stored is deleted because a plan ended: it stays viewable and exportable. If the household uses more than its allowance, new photos, videos and documents cannot be added online until it uses less or a plan is restarted; everything else in TUITEA keeps working. Items you delete yourself are removed after 7 days, and deleting your account removes what belongs to it, as described below.

Ending it

You can delete your account at any time from Your account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Records shared with your household stay with the household, and a household you started passes to its longest-standing member.
