# Store conversion improvement: "Logo Bundle" + free-shipping bar (spec, draft)

**Drafted only. No Shopify changes were made.** Source of the numbers: a read-only look at the live store on 2026-11-05.

## What exists today
- Shopify store "KEEPITIL" on the Basic plan, 323 products, all print-on-demand. Merch ships at a **flat $6.69**; digital items have no shipping. The /shop page on keepitil.com links out to Shopify checkout.
- Flagship items: Logo Tee $34, Logo Hat $30, Logo Crewneck $55, Logo Hoodie $64 (2XL $70.50).
- Flat shipping on a single $30 hat is about 22% on top of the price, which is a likely drop-off point.

## Proposal (one improvement, two parts)
1. **Logo Bundle: Tee + Hat for $59** (separate prices total $64, so $5 off). Shown as a card on /shop and on the tee and hat pages.
2. **Free-shipping bar** at checkout and on /shop: free merch shipping at **$75** (the flat $6.69 stays below that). Tee + Hat at $59 is $16 short; adding a $24-ish item or upgrading to the Crewneck bundle gets there, which nudges the basket up.

## Check before anyone turns it on
- **Margin.** Print-on-demand cost per item is not in this draft. Confirm the $59 bundle and the $75 threshold still leave margin after the $6.69 the store would stop collecting.
- **Mechanics.** Basic plan supports automatic discounts and shipping rates by order price. A true "buy X and Y" bundle discount may need an app or a bundle product; the lowest-risk start is a discount code or a single bundle product with a size selector for each item.
- **Measure.** Compare average order value and checkout completion for two weeks before and after.

## Joe decisions
- Approve or change the $59 price and $75 threshold.
- Choose automatic discount or a bundle product.
