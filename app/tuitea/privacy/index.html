TUITEA Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE ON APPROVAL]

TUITEA is a private app for one family. This policy says what it stores, where,

and who can reach it. It describes the app as actually built — every claim below

was checked against the code and the database, not written from intent.

Who we are

TUITEA is operated by KEEPITIL. Contact: info@keepitil.com.

What TUITEA stores

Your account. An email address, and a session token on your device. TUITEA

signs you in with your email address and a password. Password reset links are sent to your email.

Your household. The people you add, their names, relationships, dates and

notes, and who is a member of which household.

What you record. Memories and their photos, family history, meals,

groceries, plans, routines, habits, sleep, and health entries you enter.

Health data. If you allow it, TUITEA reads data from Apple Health. That

permission is Apple's to grant and yours to revoke at any time in iOS Settings.

TUITEA never sells health data and never shares it for advertising.

Your conversations with Tupu. What you say or type, and what Tupu replies,

so the conversation continues across sessions.

Photos and media. Some media currently lives only on your device. Where that

is true, TUITEA cannot restore it if the app is removed. This is stated plainly

because it is true today.

Who can see it

Members of your household see what is shared with the household. Nobody outside

it does. TUITEA does not sell personal data, and does not use family content for

advertising.

Service providers. TUITEA uses Supabase for its database, storage and

authentication, and one or more AI providers to power Tupu. What you send to

Tupu is processed by that provider to produce a reply.

Children

TUITEA is designed for families and expects a parent or guardian to create and

manage records about a child. Those records belong to the household.

Your choices

  • See and edit what is stored, in the app.
  • Delete a record; deletion asks first.
  • Revoke Apple Health access in iOS Settings.
  • Request account deletion from Your account → Delete account. TUITEA emails you

to confirm before anything is removed.

  • Ask for a copy of your data at info@keepitil.com.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it, or until you ask for your account to be removed.

Changes

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
