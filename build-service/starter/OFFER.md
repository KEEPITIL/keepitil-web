## Optional care plan: $29 per month

**What it costs:** $29 per month, billed monthly. Cancel any time; there is no refund for the current month.

**What it covers:**
- Checks that your site and its contact form are working.
- Up to 30 minutes per month of small text and photo changes.

**What it does not cover:** new pages, a redesign, new features, and the fees you pay your host and domain provider. Anything beyond the 30 minutes, or outside this list, is quoted in writing before any work starts.

## Hosting and your site files

**Hosting and your domain are yours.** You hold the accounts and pay your host and domain provider directly. KEEPITIL does not host your site. We build it, help you publish it, and hand you the finished site files and the `site.json` that makes them.

## Payment and deposit terms

- **50% to start, 50% before launch.** After you choose a package, we email you an invoice or payment link for the first 50%. Work starts when it is paid.
- **Cancel before work starts:** the deposit is refunded in full.
- **After we deliver the first draft:** the deposit is not refundable.
- **Balance:** due before launch. We hand over the finished site when the balance is paid.
- **If we cannot deliver what your package promises,** we refund the unfinished portion of what you paid.
- The care plan is billed separately, monthly, as above.

## What every site includes

- Mobile-first, readable at phone width, with dark and light modes.
- No trackers, no ads, no third-party scripts, no web fonts.
- Title, description, canonical URL and social preview set for every page.
- Accessible by default: skip link, visible focus, 44 px tap targets, reduced-motion respected.

## How it works

1. **Brief (day 0).** You send the details listed below. We confirm the package and date in writing.
2. **Deposit.** The first 50% starts the work.
3. **First draft** (day 3 Starter, day 6 Standard, day 9 Pro). A private preview link you can open on your phone.
4. **Revisions.** Send all feedback in one message per round; we apply it within 2 business days.
5. **Launch check.** Lighthouse on mobile (95+ on all four scores), form test, links test.
6. **Balance and go-live.** You pay the balance; we help you publish and confirm the live URL.

## What you provide

- Business name, one-line tagline, and a short description.
- Contact email (and phone or address if you want them shown).
- Your logo (PNG or SVG) and two brand colours, or tell us and we pick.
- Text for each page, or notes we can turn into text (Pro).
- Photos you own or have the right to use. We do not supply stock photos.
- Your domain name and host login, or permission for us to guide you through setting them up.
- One person who can approve changes.

## Revisions

A **revision round** is one batch of changes sent together. Wording, colour, image and layout tweaks inside the agreed pages count as revisions, up to the rounds in your package. **Not a revision:** new pages, a new design direction after sign-off, new features such as shops or logins, or content that arrives after launch. These, and extra rounds, are quoted in writing before work starts.

## Not included

Online shops or payments, member logins, custom databases, multi-language sites, SEO retainers and ad management. Ask and we will quote them as a separate project.
