KEEPITIL PRESENTS

crooki3 — The Inland Empire's Closing-Time Specialist

Resident at Club Rattlers, closer of techno stages and afters across the Inland Empire — and headed for the Bass Invaders main stage.

KEEPITIL

Read her Instagram bio the way a promoter reads it, and it isn’t a bio at all — it’s a calendar. Three dates, three rooms: 10.10 at Club Rattlers, 10.23 at Dave & Buster’s Ontario, 10.24 at 4th & Mill. No slogan, no genre list, just where crooki3 will be and when. That is the whole story of Rachelle Anne Crook of Lake Elsinore in one glance: a house and techno DJ whose career, right now, is a run of Inland Empire rooms — and whose name keeps landing in the slots that close the night out.

The next one is the biggest of the three. On October 23 she plays the main stage at Bass Invaders, the EDM night staged inside Dave & Buster’s in Ontario — “Catch me at the main stage for Bass Invaders on October 23rd!” she posted on October 8, tickets through @bass_invadersofficial, 18+. Bass Invaders bills itself as “EDM • HOUSE • TECHNO • BASS” from Southern California, and this is not her first time on its stage: in July she was back at the same Dave & Buster’s room closing out its techno stage at midnight — “CATCH ME CLOSING OUT THE TECHNO STAGE @12AM” — and her recap of that night is pure gratitude: “it was amazing catching up with familiar faces and meeting so many new people. You all brought that incredible festival energy!”

Closing the techno stage at midnight. Closing the afters at 2 a.m. Holding the 11-to-12 hour at her residency. Slot by slot, the pattern is the point: crooki3 is the DJ this circuit trusts with the end of the night.

A resident, not a guest

Club Rattlers — “Cali Natives’ House Experiences,” run by CEO @951_chico out of The Champion in Murrieta — says it outright in its bio: “Resident DJ’s: @crooki3official.” Residency is the underground’s real currency, and hers is documented across the crew’s whole first season. When Rattlers threw its first event of 2026 at The Champion, the flyer ran open decks 7–9 and crooki3 11–12. In May she was on the same room’s bill again, 11 p.m. to midnight. In June she went back-to-back with 951_chico himself for a 90-minute set — 12:30 to 2 a.m., the deepest slot on a “HOUSE MUSIC ALL NIGHT” bill at 39400 Murrieta Hot Springs Rd, free entry. Her own reels from the room say what the flyers can’t: “Always the best time @clubrattlers @the_champion_murrieta.”

And the Rattlers connection travels. In August 2025 she played Avalon Hollywood back-to-back with 951_chico — “Thanks for dancing with us LA” — on a three-day weekend that also took her to Newport. A residency that exports its resident to Hollywood is doing something right, and so is the resident.

The afters shift

If the clubs are her day job, the afters are her signature. El Gato Rojo — the Inland Empire afters run through @el.gato_rojo, with locations dropped to followers — books her long and late: a two-hour back-to-back with SergCat in May 2026 (“SERGCAT X CROOKI3 LET’S GOOOOO — 2 HR B2B WHO’S READYYYY”), and a 12:30–2 a.m. solo slot on another weekend, after the pregame’s open decks wrapped at Sushi Mamba in Wildomar. A third El Gato Rojo bill ran SergCat x crooki3 from 1 to 3 a.m.

Her words about that room explain why she keeps getting the call. After one May set she posted: “Thank you for having me and being such a safe space for creativity and growth!” — and, about a different night in the same run, the most PLUR review an afters has ever earned: “Y’ALL NEVER SEEN AN AFTERS LIKE THIS IN THE IE!! … vibes, venue, and hospitality were 100 + them constantly asking if we needed water is such a small but significant thing #PLUR.” That’s not a DJ collecting a fee; that’s a DJ who is part of the room’s culture — and who says so, on the record, in her own feed.

The circuit

Around the residency and the afters, the map fills in. Downtown Riverside: a basement set at Aurea Vista Night Club in April 2026, billed 11:45 p.m.–12:45 a.m. on a LoKo Bird Collective night, and a Funday House Sessions night at The W at Worthington’s where she posted “Good vibes and good times all night long.” Redlands: PROJEKT, where LoKo Bird brought her back repeatedly — the NIGHTWAVE takeover on January 16 alongside Mikey Quest and SallyThatGirl (9 p.m. to close, free entry), and a St. Patrick’s Day date in March. Rialto: Uptown Downtown, on a Cream Sundays Wednesday, where she wrote “LOVE the spot, the people, and the vibes!!!” — and posed for the portrait on this page under the room’s neon sign. Rancho Cucamonga: 4th & Mill, where she played the Halloween EDM party in 2025 and where her bio has her returning on October 24, the night after Bass Invaders. Pomona and a studio date closed out a December 2025 week: “Come dance with me this coming week — 12.11 @flexxpomona, 12.14 @rendezvoustudios.”

She has also already played a KEEPITIL room. On June 6, 2026, KEEPITIL’s own account posted the patio night at Bleu Nightclub in Westminster — Casual Hippies x KEEPITIL, “a curated night of original sounds, smooth grooves, and an intimate open-air atmosphere” — with a lineup of CROOKI3, SWERVE and DOC MARIO, billed as “a refined blend of rhythm and energy designed for those who appreciate the deeper side of house music,” with open decks before it and a techno room running all night. She applied to this platform in June 2026. She had, in a sense, already played for it.

The sound

Her application splits it four ways: house, tech/bass/latin house, techno. The bookings prove each word of that. The Rattlers bills are house music all night; the Bass Invaders stage is techno at midnight; the hashtags on her Club Rattlers reels read #techhouse; the Bleu patio was the deeper side of house. And then there is the livestream chapter: in April 2026, C.R.E.A.M. Sundays — the crew behind the C.R.E.A.M. Studios livestream, “Global House | Deep House | Disco House” — put her on Episode 004 of the stream alongside Blerry, Cisco (MX), WhereYourNot and CL Audio, promising “deep grooves, unexpected turns, and raw creativity from start to finish.” Her own Cream Sundays reel, captioned “This one is for the ladies,” is a DJ having fun with a room that knows her.

What ties the range together is feel rather than genre loyalty. Her most-liked reel of the summer is a Club Rattlers clip captioned: “That drop hit so hard I forgot I was the one playing it — I went through all the phases like it was the first time.” Promoters can book a lot of things; a DJ who still reacts to her own drop like a fan is the thing they book twice.

Why book crooki3

  • A proven closer: midnight techno-stage close at Bass Invaders (Dave & Buster’s Ontario, July 2026), 12:30–2 a.m. and 1–3 a.m. afters slots with El Gato Rojo, and a main-stage return at Bass Invaders on October 23, 2026
  • A resident with a home room: Club Rattlers’ named resident DJ at The Champion, Murrieta — with documented back-to-back chemistry with the crew’s own 951_chico, including at Avalon Hollywood
  • Range on one application line: house, tech house, bass house and Latin house through to techno — club sets, patio sets, basement sets and a Twitch livestream (C.R.E.A.M. Studios Episode 004) all on the record
  • A circuit that spans the IE and beyond: Lake Elsinore base; Murrieta, Ontario, Riverside, Redlands, Rialto, Rancho Cucamonga, Pomona, Westminster and Hollywood on her recent map
  • She promotes the rooms that book her — recap reels, thank-you posts and a bio that is literally her next three dates

The quick version

crooki3 (Rachelle Anne Crook) is a Lake Elsinore house, tech/bass/Latin house and techno DJ, resident at Club Rattlers in Murrieta, a repeat closer on the Inland Empire’s afters circuit with El Gato Rojo, and a main-stage act at Bass Invaders inside Dave & Buster’s Ontario — where she closed the techno stage at midnight in July 2026 and returns to the main stage on October 23. Recent rooms run from PROJEKT Redlands and Aurea Vista Riverside to Avalon Hollywood and KEEPITIL’s own Bleu patio night in Westminster, with a C.R.E.A.M. Studios livestream set in between.
