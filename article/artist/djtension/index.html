DJ Tensión — Building the Valley's Groove From the Inside

Founder of Aztecha Groovers and Summer Bopz Radio, resident at Secret SFV — a Van Nuys DJ who builds the platforms, then plays them.

KEEPITIL

Most DJ bios are a list of rooms played. Leonardo Villalobos’s reads like an org chart. “Founder: @aztechagroovers @summerbopzradio. Resident: @secret_sfv.” Before you get to a single set, DJ Tensión — of Van Nuys, in the San Fernando Valley — has already told you what he actually does: he builds the platforms, then he plays them. A collective. A radio show that turned into live pop-ups. A residency with the Valley-and-DTLA events crew Secret SFV. And in between, a working circuit of DTLA bars, Long Beach afters and Hollywood showcases, all of it in the minimal, deep and Latin tech lane he put on his application.

The newest piece launched in May 2026. Aztecha Groovers introduced itself as “the San Fernando Valleys Newest Underground Music Collective,” with a mission statement that doubles as his: “Our Goal is to provide real groovers and music lovers with the sounds inspired by Miami Terrace & Ibiza Nightclubs. We are focused heavily on bringing you Podcasts Mixes, Underground Events, and Music Releases from local SFV/LA/OC/IE Artist.” Its own bio shortens that to “Sounds from Miami Terrace + Ibiza Nightclubs.” That’s the Tensión thesis in one line — take the terrace groove of Miami and Ibiza, and play it in the 818.

The sound

His application lists it as “Minimal / Deep / Latin / Tech / House”; his Instagram bio compresses it to “Minimal / Deep / Latin Tech.” The bookings line up behind both. In Long Beach in June 2025 he opened the night — 11 p.m. to midnight — for SoundTech OC at Fuerte, on a bill he billed himself as “a night filled with Latin Tech / Tech / House,” alongside Ogawa, Bay Area headliner Alec Cortez and local talent Daren DePalma, 6dank and Alcantar Music, doors at 11 and “going all night long.” At Pattern Bar in DTLA in March 2025, his Sunday day-party set sat inside “7 hours of Latin House.” At Blind Tiger’s Nightshift in DTLA, he’s an afters booking. The genre words change order from flyer to flyer; the groove doesn’t.

And he knows exactly what he is not. Thanking Blind Tiger for an opening slot at its Hollywood Showcase at The Room in August 2026, he added, in caps and parentheses: “(NO I DID NOT PLAY BAD BUNNY).” It’s a joke, and it’s also a mission statement — the Latin in his Latin tech comes from the underground, not the pop charts.

Founder, twice

Summer Bopz Radio came first: a community DJ podcast and mix series whose own page thanks “all of our listeners & viewers for constantly supporting and helping create this loving community based DJ podcast mix.” In September 2025 the show stepped outside — Summer Bopz Radio @ The Yard in Van Nuys, a Sunday session (12–8 p.m., secret location announced the day of) with live DJs, vendors, open decks and vinyl DJs, brought together with @mx.marrr. Villalobos was on the lineup, and he also supplied the live DJ equipment, with @plaiimusikrekordz. In November 2025 came the MAR x Summer Bopz Radio pop-up at Woodley Park — his recap thanks everyone “who came out to support our first pop up event,” and credits the crew who made a last-minute venue change work: generator, vinyl equipment, speaker system, all homie-supplied. His YouTube channel bio carries the same credit line: “DJ & Producer — Founder of Summer Bopz Radio — LA / SFV.”

Aztecha Groovers, launched May 2026, is the bigger swing: not just a show but a collective, openly recruiting — “We are building a loving community and searching for Content Creators, Promoters, and Media” — with demos, mixes and collabs taken at aztechagroovers.us@gmail.com, and genres wanted listed as House, Deep House, Latin Tech, Deep Tech, Minimal Tech and Tech House. It is still a small account with a large blueprint. But the pattern is established: where other DJs wait for a platform, Tensión files the paperwork and builds one.

The residency and the circuit

The third line of the bio is the anchor: resident at Secret SFV, the “SFV and DTLA Dance Events” crew (3,269 followers, powered by Bass Boss) behind a run of Valley and downtown nights. His Compound by Dirt Dog sets run through it — a January 2026 back-to-back with Vic Sosa (“Beer on Tap + The Best LA Hot Dogs + House Music All Night Long,” free before 9:30), an Illumination glow rave at the same room in April, and a February 2026 post from the room: “One hell of a night brought to you by @secret_sfv.” In June 2026 he was back in a park with the crew — Inner Circle LA x Secret SFV at Woodley Park, “The Return of the Grooves,” back-to-back with Jay Lyric — documented in his DJ Vlog 003.

The vlogs are part of the craft now: DJ Vlog 001 recapped a Plur Eventz night at The Compound; Vlog 002 was a goodbye — Pattern Bar’s last Saturday after 15 years, where he played an impromptu back-to-back with Necio and eulogised the room properly: “Tons of LA locals know the history behind DTLAs most known unknown bar but if you didn’t this was a staple for most DJs or Collectives just starting off.” His own history with that room runs back to the Latin House day party in 2025. Rooms close; the DJs who document them keep their history.

Around the Valley core, the wider circuit is documented room by room: opening Blind Tiger’s Hollywood Showcase at The Room; the Nightshift afters in DTLA (“Dm me for info or link in bio for RSVP (Free Before 1am)”); Valley House Call at Dark Horse Tavern, where the hosts introduced him as “@leovillalobos (DJ Tension) of @secret_sfv and @summerbopzradio throwing down on the 1s and 2s with his immaculate vibes,” on a night billed as showcasing “the most talented DJs the valley has to offer”; a Wet Dreams pool party in Granada Hills (Secret SFV x Wrkshp Events, hosted by Sounds by Log, July 2025); Melrose House, where his story highlights have him opening the outside stage and going back-to-back with Kaliente for SoundPeak Events. And going back to June 2024, a closing set at madgallery.la for Tempo Lab that he captioned with unusual honesty: “Played my first show since my surgery. I can’t wait to get better so I can play longer slots.” He got better. The slots got longer.

His own advice, posted in April 2026, is the ethic under all of it: “Your favorite DJs had to start somewhere right? Support your local underground artist. Keep going and don’t give up. Go make your dreams a reality!” Tensión doesn’t just say it — the open decks at his own events, the equipment he lends his own radio show, and a collective recruiting promoters and media alongside DJs are what saying it looks like in practice.

Why book DJ Tensión

  • Two platforms of his own: founder of Aztecha Groovers (SFV underground collective, launched May 2026) and Summer Bopz Radio (community podcast/mix series turned live pop-up brand) — a booking that comes with its own audience-building machinery
  • A residency that anchors him: resident DJ for Secret SFV, the SFV/DTLA events crew, with repeat sets at The Compound by Dirt Dog in solo and back-to-back formats
  • A defined, bookable sound: minimal / deep / Latin tech and house — opening set for SoundTech OC’s Latin tech night in Long Beach, a Latin House day party at Pattern Bar, DTLA afters at Nightshift
  • Slot range: opener (Blind Tiger Hollywood Showcase; 11 p.m.–12 a.m. in Long Beach), day-party and pool-party sets (Granada Hills; Woodley Park), park pop-ups, and a documented closing set (madgallery.la)
  • Self-documenting: his DJ Vlog series recaps the rooms he plays — your event gets content, not just a set
  • Plugged into the Valley scene end to end: Van Nuys base, Valley House Call at Dark Horse Tavern, Secret SFV, Inner Circle LA — plus DTLA, Hollywood and Long Beach receipts

The quick version

DJ Tensión (Leonardo Villalobos) is a Van Nuys DJ playing minimal, deep and Latin tech and house across the San Fernando Valley and Los Angeles. He is the founder of the Aztecha Groovers collective and of Summer Bopz Radio — a community podcast that has grown into live pop-ups at Van Nuys and Woodley Park — and a resident DJ for Secret SFV, with documented sets at The Compound by Dirt Dog, Pattern Bar, Blind Tiger’s Nightshift and Hollywood Showcase, Fuerte in Long Beach, Dark Horse Tavern and Melrose House — plus mixes on SoundCloud, sets on YouTube and streams on Twitch, all linked from his Linktree.
