GalacticGumby — The Inland Empire's Speed Garage Selecta

San Jacinto’s Joel Mondragon — a speed garage/UKG selecta with a residency pattern at Projekt Redlands, W Riverside lobby slots, and a barber’s chair and a Twitch channel besides.

KEEPITIL

Some DJs launch with a logo, a press kit and a plan. GalacticGumby launched with a jam session. The first post on @press_play_gumby — the dedicated music account of San Jacinto’s Joel Mondragon — is a clip from February 2026 captioned, in full: “Having fun during my jam session and using this as my first clip/post hope you enjoy and more to come.” No hype copy, no announcement. Just a working DJ, a pair of decks, and an open invitation to listen. Eight months later, that account’s receipts tell the story of one of the Inland Empire’s busiest underground regulars: speed garage and UKG at a skate shop’s anniversary, house in the lobby of the W in Riverside, techno at a brewery in Ontario, and a standing Tuesday-night home at Projekt Redlands that the flyers keep coming back to.

GalacticGumby is the DJ identity Mondragon built alongside — not instead of — everything else in his feed. His main account, @galactic_gumby (1,278 followers, 632 posts), points straight at it: the bio routes music followers to @press_play_gumby and streams to Twitch, and it also carries the other trade in his hands, barbering, via @gumby_cuts_hair. The music account is where the work is documented. Its bio is the whole pitch in five lines: “Welcome, let’s listen to some tunes — Speed garage / HardHouse/house / Techno — DJ/music account — Spinnin a lil of everything — Tunes On Twitch.”

Two accounts, one DJ

The split is deliberate and worth understanding, because it is how the bookings actually happen. @galactic_gumby is the person — the account promoters tag, the account with the years of history on it. @press_play_gumby is the act: four posts deep at research time, every one of them a performance clip, with the Twitch link (twitch.tv/DJ_GalacticGumby) in the bio for the livestream side of the project. When RaveX IE and Knight Skate had him spin, the thank-you went on the music account. When Vibe Junkies booked him, same. The personal account carries the flyers and the community; the music account carries the proof of work.

That community is the Inland Empire’s underground in miniature. The names around him recur like a repertory cast: Vibe Junkies, Planet Techno California, Sinergy Entertainment, RaveX IE, Funday House Sessions. He is not a touring name dropping into these rooms — he is part of the furniture, and the flyers prove it month after month.

The sound: garage first, but “a lil of everything”

His application to KEEPITIL lists the full range: speed garage/UKG, house, hard house, techno, hard techno. The music account’s bio leads with speed garage, and the clips back the billing. His Knight Skate set is tagged #speedgarage #ukg #selecta; his Vibe Junkies clip is tagged #ukg #speedgarage #dj. “Selecta” is the right word for the approach — this is UK garage culture, where the DJ’s job is the tune selection and the reload-worthy moment, played out in rooms that reward energy over ego.

But the range in the application is real, not résumé inflation. The same DJ who plays garage at the skate shop played a techno set for Planet Techno California at Projekt Redlands in May 2025 — his own caption for that one is the most honest debut line in his feed: “Babys first techno set. Thank you @planettechnocalifornia for the opportunity I had a blast!!” A year later Planet Techno’s Black Mass lineup at the same venue listed him on the outdoor stage in a run of back-to-back sets billed as “Techno & House vibes,” and by January 2026 the crew’s house-and-techno night at Metabolic Brewing in Ontario was announcing “Sound system ready. Drinks flowing. Underground energy all night.” with Galactic Gumby in its orbit. A garage DJ who can be trusted with a techno stage, and a techno-capable DJ whose home genre is UKG — that combination is exactly what small-room promoters in the IE are short of.

The circuit: Redlands, Riverside, Ontario

Projekt Redlands is the home room. Vibe Junkies’ Tuesday nights there have carried his name again and again: a March 10, 2026 date; an August 2026 bill alongside Its Nihilo, Dawliciouss, DJ Treesap, The Real King Pins and Kimibex (“See you tomorrow — come catch a vibe with our djs for the night, this Tuesday in downtown Redlands”); a September 2026 Tuesday with The Millennium Music, Casa Dola and Car Car Mixes; an October 2026 Sunday bill; and a “UKG + DNB this Tuesday” flyer in October 2026. That is not a guest spot — that is a residency pattern, earned one Tuesday at a time in a downtown Redlands bar room.

Riverside is the second home. In March 2026, Funday House Sessions’ “EROTICA RETURNS” took over The W Riverside with three stages and two patios of non-stop house music — open air, lobby and indoor rooms — and GalacticGumby held a lobby slot on a bill with JSnax, Xhakraw, LilMatthew DLR, Louuu Dawg and Gingerbeerdman. The same month, Sinergy Entertainment’s takeover of the W lobby listed him on its stage, and Sinergy’s Sunday Reset series — the Beyond Wonderland recovery party edition included — put him on a house, minimal and Latin bill (“Pull up. Reset. Run it back.”) with open decks before the main run. Open decks matter here: this is a scene that keeps its doors open to the next DJ, and Gumby plays both sides of that door — booked name and community regular.

Then there are the rooms that show the range. Knight Skate — the San Bernardino skate shop whose sixth anniversary RaveX IE soundtracked in August 2026, music running 6 p.m. to 11 p.m. after a day of skate sessions — got a speed garage set; his recap is pure gratitude: “Thank you @ravex.ie & @crowqui for having me spin for knight skate had a blast.” The Kitsch Bar got a curated night of “house, disco & UKG” in June 2026. His story highlights carry a thank-you to Space Taco Tuesdays, Party Pat, Fuchie Events and V Lounge Fox “for having me.” Skate shops, breweries, hotel lobbies, bar rooms and Tuesday nights — it is the working circuit of the Inland Empire underground, and he is on all of it.

The other trades

The main account explains where the stagecraft comes from. Mondragon is a trained barber — the feed documents barber school in 2022 (“Last full day of school today. Graduate on monday”), graduation that December (“Mission complete!”), and a working chair after it, from quiffhawks and mid-fades to volunteering cuts for West Valley High School students getting ready for prom in 2023. The @gumby_cuts_hair handle sits at the top of his bio, first line, ahead of the music. He is also a Twitch streamer — the galactic_gumby channel predates the DJ clips, with gaming streams going back years — and the DJ project inherited that instinct: “Tunes On Twitch” is the music account’s standing offer, sets streamed under DJ_GalacticGumby for anyone who can’t make it to Redlands on a Tuesday.

None of that is decoration. A barber-DJ-streamer from San Jacinto brings three communities to every booking, and the rooms booking him — skate shops and breweries, not superclubs — are exactly the rooms where a DJ who is genuinely of the place outdraws a bigger name passing through.

Why book GalacticGumby

  • A genuine speed garage / UKG selecta — a sound with heat right now and very few working specialists in the Inland Empire; his clips and hashtags lead with it because it is the headline act
  • Range the flyers verify: house (W Riverside lobby, Funday House Sessions; Sunday Reset), hard house, techno and hard techno (Planet Techno California, Black Mass outdoor stage) — one booking covers a whole night’s arc
  • A residency pattern at Projekt Redlands with Vibe Junkies — repeat Tuesday and Sunday bookings across 2025–2026, the clearest reliability signal a small-room DJ can show
  • Community draw across three audiences: the IE underground, the skate scene (Knight Skate anniversary), and a Twitch following that gets “Tunes On Twitch” streams under DJ_GalacticGumby
  • Room-agnostic: skate shop, brewery (Metabolic Brewing, Ontario), hotel lobby, bar room — he plays the IE’s real circuit, not just its clubs

The quick version

GalacticGumby is Joel Mondragon of San Jacinto: a speed garage/UKG-first DJ — house, hard house, techno and hard techno all in the bag — holding a repeat-booking pattern at Projekt Redlands with Vibe Junkies, lobby and stage slots in Riverside with Funday House Sessions and Sinergy Entertainment, techno bills with Planet Techno California, and a garage set at Knight Skate’s sixth anniversary. A barber and a Twitch streamer besides, he documents it all on @press_play_gumby, the music account he launched in February 2026 with a jam session and the words “more to come.” More came.
