BLEU — Two Rooms on Beach Blvd, Built for the Moments That Matter

A full-service, two-room event space in Westminster — 150 seated, up to 400 standing — at the same Beach Blvd address as Bleu Restaurant and its Bleu Night Club nights.

KEEPITIL

Every neighborhood has an address that quietly does more than the sign outside suggests. On Beach Blvd in Westminster, that address is 14160 — and the name on the room is BLEU.

By day, the building feeds Little Saigon: Bleu Restaurant serves Vietnamese cuisine with a full bar, the kind of place where bún bò huế and phở share a menu with cocktails. But the same address carries a second life. As an event space, BLEU is a full-service, two-room venue — “an intimate two-room event space for the moments that matter,” in its own words — built for celebrations that feel effortless. And after dark, the room has a working nightclub résumé: promoter nights, live performances, and DJ bills run through the same doors under the Bleu Restaurant & Dancing name.

That range is exactly why BLEU sits on KEEPITIL Connect as a venue — and why artists on this platform talk about it the way they do. In his KEEPITIL feature, SWERVE names headlining at BLEU as a goal — the kind of room, as his article puts it, where a DJ’s personality, precision, and stamina all matter. Rooms earn reputations like that one booked night at a time.

One address, two lives

BLEU’s official description is simple: “a full-service event space built for celebrations that feel effortless. Two flexible rooms, a connected outdoor patio, and an on-site planning team mean you can host anything from an intimate dinner to a 400-person night — with sound, lighting, and coordination already handled.”

That is the private-events side. The public side is just as busy. Eventbrite listings for the same address — billed as Bleu Restaurant & Dancing, or simply Bleu Night Club — show a steady run of ticketed nights: THE PURGE, a pre-Halloween costume party with four DJs on October 16; a NEON RAVE on September 25; a WHIPLASH hyperpop dance party on October 29; a Summer Kick Off party in June 2025; and a live performance by Bino Rideaux in March. Vivid Seats, which sells tickets for the room, describes it as part restaurant, part nightclub, with a spacious dance floor, a raised stage, immersive sound and intelligent lighting, and a calendar leaning into Top 40, hip-hop, EDM and international hits.

In other words: this is not a banquet hall that occasionally rents itself out. It’s a room that already works — as a restaurant, as a club, and as a private event space — and BLEU is the name on the event-space side of that operation.

The two rooms

The venue is split into two flexible rooms, and the pricing is published and straightforward:

  • The Small Room holds up to 150 guests — $1,500 Monday–Thursday, $2,000 Friday/Sunday, $2,500 Saturday.
  • The Large Room seats 250 or holds up to 400 standing — $2,000 Monday–Thursday, $2,500 Friday/Sunday, $3,000 Saturday.

Across both rooms, the venue’s headline capacity is 150 seated and up to 400 standing. Every booking includes five event hours plus two setup hours, with additional time at $250 an hour — a structure that tells you this team runs events on a real schedule, not a handshake and a prayer.

What’s already handled

The pitch of a full-service space lives or dies on the inclusions list, and BLEU’s is long. Every booking comes with an on-site event planner and an on-site coordinator, tables and chairs, lounge couches, linens, a sound system, lighting, TVs, security, cleaning, coolers and ice, and the connected outdoor patio. Music playback is covered too — and if you’re bringing your own DJ, the venue’s FAQ is explicit: you’re welcome to. Sound, lighting, and Spotify are included; the booth is yours.

The flexibility extends to the bar and the menu. Outside food and outside vendors are welcome for a flat $150 fee. A corkage bar setup is available, with a bartender at $150 flat. And if you want to see the room before you commit, tours are offered through the booking request — the BLEU team reviews each request and follows up to confirm availability, pricing, and a tour.

The calendar it holds

BLEU’s listed event types read like a map of how Orange County actually celebrates: weddings and receptions, birthdays and quinceañeras, corporate events and mixers, concerts and DJ nights, and private parties. Westminster sits in the middle of Little Saigon and a short drive from Garden Grove, Anaheim, and the beach cities — a crossroads position that suits a room booking both family-scale celebrations and 400-person club nights.

The club side of the calendar proves the room can hold the second kind. The Eventbrite bills at this address run 10 PM to 2 AM, 18+, with multi-DJ lineups and themed production — costume parties, neon raves, hyperpop nights — plus live artist performances like Bino Rideaux’s March show. Promoters including College Parties OC and TheToxicParties have put their names on nights here, which is its own kind of credential: outside promoters don’t return to rooms that don’t work.
