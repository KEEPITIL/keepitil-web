TUITEA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

TUITEA is a family app made by KEEPITIL. This policy describes the app as it is actually built. Every statement below was checked against the code and the database rather than written from intent, and where something is only partly true it says so.

Who we are

TUITEA is operated by KEEPITIL. Contact: info@keepitil.com.

What TUITEA stores

Your account. Your email address, and a session on your device. You sign in with an email address and a password. Password reset links go to your email.

Your household. The people you add, their names, relationships, dates and notes, and who belongs to which household.

What you record. Memories and their photos, family history, meals, groceries, plans, routines, habits, sleep, and any health entries you type in yourself.

Health data from Apple Health. Only if you turn it on. This has its own page: Health & Apple Health.

Your conversations with Tupu. What you ask, and what Tupu answers, so a conversation continues across sessions. These are kept in your own account in TUITEA's database, protected by row-level security so that only you can read them. Storing a conversation is not the same as sending it: what is stored is described here, and what may be sent to an AI provider is described under "Tupu and AI providers" below.

Photos and media. Some media currently lives only on your device. Where that is true, TUITEA cannot restore it if the app is removed. This is stated plainly because it is true today.

Who can see it

Members of your household see what is shared with the household; a record you keep private stays private to you. Nobody outside your household sees any of it. TUITEA does not sell personal data and does not use family content for advertising.

Service providers. TUITEA uses Supabase for its database, storage and authentication.

Tupu and AI providers

Many of Tupu's answers never reach an AI provider at all: questions about your own family, your schedule and your recorded health readings are answered from your own records on the spot.

When a question does need a model, what may be sent is decided by a provider registry that records, for each provider, which classes of data it is approved for, whether it retains data, and whether it trains on data. A provider that has not been approved for a class of data does not receive it, and an unverified provider is treated as not approved rather than as approved.

The current provider is Cloudflare Workers AI, approved for general and personal content. Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum is incorporated into its Self-Serve Subscription Agreement, so it applies to this account.

Health information is in a stricter class that no remote provider is approved for. See Health & Apple Health for the exceptions and the rule that governs them.

Children

TUITEA is built for families and expects a parent or guardian to create and manage records about a child. Those records belong to the household.

Your choices

  • See and edit what is stored, in the app.
  • Delete a record. TUITEA asks first.
  • Turn Apple Health off in TUITEA, or revoke it in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account, from Your account → Delete account. This deletes it immediately: your account and the records only you own are removed at once, and it cannot be undone. Records you shared with your household stay with the household. If you started a household, it passes to the member who has been there longest so that nobody else loses access.
  • Ask for a copy of your data at info@keepitil.com.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it, or until you delete your account.

Changes

Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect.
