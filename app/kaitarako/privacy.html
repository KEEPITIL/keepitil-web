Kai Tarako is a free letter-tracing app for young children, made by KEEPITIL. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with information. The short version: we don’t collect anything. Kai Tarako has no accounts, and nothing your child does in the app is sent to us or to anyone else. It stays on the device.

What we collect

Nothing. Kai Tarako has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. It never asks for a name, age, email, photo, voice recording or location. It does not use the camera, the microphone, location, contacts or notifications, and it asks for no device permissions.

What stays on your device

So your child can pick up where they left off, the app saves a small amount of information only on this device:

  • the settings a grown-up chooses (help level, mode, starting step and similar options);
  • simple progress counts for each lesson: how many times it was finished, how often the “?” help button was tapped, and the date it was last practised;
  • a simple skill record for each letter, word or sound (for example “trace:a”): how many times it was done, how many of those needed help (a second try or tracing help), how many were right first time on their own, and the dates (day only, at most the last 60) on which it was right on their own;
  • the days each child played (dates only, at most the last 400), for the practice calendar;
  • the date progress was first saved;
  • if a grown-up adds more than one child: each child’s picture and colour (chosen from a fixed list; there is no place to type a name) and that child’s own progress, as above;
  • the Grown-ups lock end time: a timestamp only;
  • if a grown-up turns on the break reminder: how long the app has been open during this visit, kept only until the app or browser tab is closed.

The app does not save the drawings or finger paths your child makes. KEEPITIL never receives any of this information. On an iPhone, the app keeps it in its own storage on the device; like other app data, it can be included in the device’s own backups (for example iCloud or a computer backup) if you use them. In a web browser, it stays in that browser’s storage.

Nothing sent, no ads, no tracking

  • The app makes no network requests while your child uses it, and it has no account or online service to send anything to.
  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, no tracking across apps or websites and no advertising or device identifiers.
  • No third-party code, services or software kits for analytics, ads or tracking. The app is built only with open-source building blocks that run on the device (listed in the open-source notices in the Grown-ups area).
  • No in-app purchases, chat, social features or links for your child to follow.

Speech uses the device’s built-in voices only. Letters and prompts are read aloud on the device by those voices. Nothing is recorded, and no audio is sent anywhere.

If you use Kai Tarako in a web browser at keepitil.com, your browser downloads the app’s files from our website, as with any web page, and may keep a copy so the app works offline. After that, the app sends nothing about your child.

Backups and printing

In the Grown-ups area you can Save backup to make a file with the progress and settings above. You choose where it goes, using your device’s own save or share options. We never receive it. Restore backup reads such a file back in. The app checks the file and ignores anything that isn’t a Kai Tarako backup.

The printable practice sheet and the progress summary are made on the device. They go only to the printer or place you pick.

The Grown-ups area (parental gate)

Settings, backups, this policy and anything that leaves the app sit behind a grown-ups gate. To open it, press and hold the Grown-ups button for 3 seconds, then answer a times-table question (for example, 14 × 6). A wrong answer closes the gate, and after two wrong answers it locks for one minute. This keeps young children out; it is not a password and does not identify anyone. The only links inside it are to this policy and the open-source notices, and this policy’s email link.

Deleting information

  • Reset in the app: Grown-ups → Privacy → Delete all progress removes all progress and settings from this device.
  • Remove the app: deleting Kai Tarako from your device removes everything it saved.
  • Any backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

Because we hold no information about you or your child, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children’s privacy and parents’ rights

Kai Tarako is made for children 5 and under. We built it to collect no personal information from children, so there is nothing that needs a parent’s consent to collect. Children never create accounts, sign in or enter personal information.

A parent or guardian decides whether a child uses Kai Tarako. Because the app collects no personal information, KEEPITIL has no account, profile or file about your child to show you, correct or delete. On the device, a parent can:

  • Review the settings and progress described above. They are in the Grown-ups area, on this device only.
  • Delete them with Grown-ups → Privacy → Delete all progress, or by removing the app.
  • Keep or delete any backup file you saved. We never receive that file.
  • Refuse collection: the app does not collect personal information, and KEEPITIL will not start collecting it without first changing this policy and, where the law requires it, asking a parent.

Questions about your child’s use of the app: email info@keepitil.com.

Sharing

We have no information about you or your child to sell, rent or share.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. We will not start collecting information without changing this policy first and, where the law requires it, asking a parent.
