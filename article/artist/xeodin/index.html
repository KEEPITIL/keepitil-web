XEODIN — San Diego's Tearout Engine, Built for the Rail

A San Diego dubstep, tearout and riddim DJ — one sixth of 6th Sense, half of XEOPHN — with direct-support slots for Sullivan King, Stoned Level and Sisto and debuts in LA, SF and Las Vegas.

KEEPITIL

Ask the promoter who booked him for his Los Angeles debut and the description writes itself: “Making his first Rave Haven Debut Xeodin is a Dubstep/Riddim DJ based out of San Diego, California specializing in high energy, overwhelming intensity and constant delivery of chaos that is sure to keep a crowd moving from start to finish.” That was Rave Haven’s own billing in October 2026, and it matches the bio XEODIN — Christopher Dakota Gonzalez of San Diego — runs on his own page: “DUBSTEP | DJ/PRODUCER | SOCAL.” In the space of one year, that chaos has carried him from San Diego’s underground rooms to direct-support slots for Sullivan King, Stoned Level, Sisto and Eradicate, a headline set in Las Vegas, debuts in Los Angeles and San Francisco, and a Halloween-night return to San Diego on a two-day festival bill. The résumé is not old. It is just very, very dense.

The anchor fact is the support list, and it comes from the promoter’s post, not his own hype: “Xeodin has supported artists such as Finnuh, RZRKT, Sport Mode, Stoned Level, Sullivan King, SYTHYST.” For a dubstep and riddim DJ, that is the heavy end of the modern bass scene — and his own feed fills in the how, one room at a time.

One sixth of the Sense

XEODIN does not move alone. His bio carries the line “1/6 @6thsense.dubstep” — he is one of six in 6th Sense, the San Diego dubstep collective whose own motto, posted in May 2026, is “Beware the 6. Genuine dubstep. No B.S. You’re not ready for us.” The collective is the engine under the solo career: 6th Sense nights at Connect San Diego, co-bills with Sounds Good Collective, and a standing cast of brothers he plays with in every combination the format allows — solo, b2b, b3b.

The signature pairing is with DLPHN. As XEOPHN — XEODIN b2b DLPHN — the two played direct support for Onex and Moros at the Almost Human show on July 31, 2026, at Connect San Diego, the night Sounds Good Collective and 6th Sense billed as “San Diego’s biggest Riddim + Dubstep show of this summer,” built around Onex unveiling his Almost Human EP. The full XEODIN b2b DLPHN set from that night is live on SoundCloud — his caption for it is the working DJ’s promise: “Go listen to this for your next gym sesh, the commute to your 9-5, or to pregame before your next riddim function and I promise you won’t be disappointed.” The recap post is just as direct about what the slot meant: “we had the privilege to play direct support for @onex.mp3 & @morosdubz! Playing for you all was a blast.” XEOPHN returns on Halloween night 2026 for Night Fall, Zerkamania Productions and Phase One Events’ two-day San Diego event, playing support for Sisto and for Vampa b2b Badvoid.

The sound: doubles, tearout, no breath

The application lists dubstep, tearout and riddim, and the scene’s own write-ups agree on the delivery. Beyond The Bass, the Houston bass-music publication that featured him in July 2026, put it this way: “Fueled by hard-hitting bass, underground influences, and relentless stage energy, XEODIN is carving out a sound that thrives on intensity and audience connection. Every set is built to keep the crowd moving, blending crushing doubles, tearout-inspired chaos, and a performance style that keeps fans locked in from the first drop to the last.”

“Crushing doubles” is the technical heart of it. XEODIN’s reels are a working catalogue of the form — posts built around two tracks slammed together, IDs listed for the heads: Volative and Heisvicious, Hamro, Infekt, Hurtbox and Shiverz. His contest submissions show the same craft aimed at the scene’s gatekeepers: a #backinthepitsa entry for Blaize Presents’ contest to open for DJ Hamro, a #rippedinferno double submission, a Bass Invasion Podcast appearance for Paralysis Presents featuring “a 30 minute set by yours truly (that I snapped my volume fader mid-set during) featuring some of my favorite doubles and unreleased tracks from the homies.” Even the accident becomes content, because the content is the point: this is a DJ whose reputation is being built in public, clip by clip, double by double.

The circuit: San Diego first, then everywhere

The home rooms came first and they are still home. Breakpoint PB hosted his Sounds Gritty: Bass Function set for Sounds Good Collective in March 2026 (“nothing but proper dub all night long”), his direct-support set for Eradicate in November 2025 — his first time playing the room, with his own promo code “XOD” at checkout — and a 4/20 bill after that. Nova SD’s terrace hosted his Sullivan King night in August 2026, which he himself flagged as a deliberate change of pace: “We are going to be switching it up from our usual programming and bring a whole different type of vibe on the rooftop.” Connect San Diego in Ocean Beach hosted the Almost Human show and its Pyke pre-party, an art-gallery rave where the 6th Sense pairs ran the peak hours — XEODIN b2b DLPHN at midnight.

Then the map opened up, city by city, each one announced like a level unlock. Las Vegas debut, spring 2026, for Bass Surge: “thank you so much for welcoming me with such open arms and having me for my very first out of state show!” — followed by a Bass Surge Wednesdays headline slot at Audiobar in the Las Vegas Arts District, billed as “one of San Diego’s finest,” and an October 23, 2026 return to The Wall at AREA15, a b3b with DLPHN and Da Impaler in support of Stayns, via Twisted Promotions and Suffer Syndicate. Los Angeles debut, June 12, 2026, on the dubstep stage at Oblivion: Dawn of the Apocalypse — a bill he noted carried “everything from aggressive tearout, riddim, and drum n’ bass.” Huntington Beach, June 6, 2026: opening for Stoned Level at The Circle OC — “What an absolute pleasure that was opening up for the legend @stoned_level” — plus a Bass Realm b2b with Remnant at XO Lounge for Paralysis Presents. Santa Ana, May 30, 2026: a b3b with DLPHN and Dismantlz at Top Floor DTSA for Savage Entertainment, on Per Poli’s California debut. San Francisco, October 2026: the first official Dobie vs XEODIN b2b for Basswoods Events — billed, accurately, as “THIS AINT A B2B IT’S A 1V1” — and a recap that needs no editor: “Shoutout to San Francisco for insane energy during my b2b with @dobiedubz and my SF debut!!” Somewhere in there, a b2b in Texas with Dobie that both of them keep threatening to bring back to California.

The Bass Jungle run shows how some of these slots are earned. In April 2026 he posted the win plainly: “After competing in a star-studded competition packed to the brim with insane talent, I am proud and immensely excited to announce that I am playing support for none other than @rzrktmusic, @sythystmusic and @finnuh” — a competition victory converted into a support slot for three of his stated idols (“Some of my biggest idols and inspirations in the game right now”), at a Zerkamania / Phase One night in San Diego. Add the April 2026 “WELCOME TO THE SOCIETY” post from Sikeout Society and the picture is of a DJ being collected by the scene’s institutions at the same time as its promoters.

What’s on the calendar

The October 2026 post laid out the run himself: “coming hot off the amazing show in SF, we got a lot more in store for the month of October and some more shows later on to close out the year!!! beyond excited to play in these different cities.” The immediate dates: Nightmare Realm in Los Angeles — a massive two-day event from Rave Haven, Hard Havoc, Tech My Warehouse LA and Neo Bounce at The Compound LA, October 16, 2026, his Rave Haven debut — the Las Vegas b3b for Stayns at The Wall at AREA15 on October 23, and Night Fall in San Diego on Halloween night, XEOPHN supporting Sisto and Vampa b2b Badvoid. His bio links carry the ticket links for the Vegas and Halloween dates, because the other thing the feed proves is that he promotes what he plays: ticket links, promo codes and “grab yours before they’re gone” are as much a part of the act as the doubles.

Why book XEODIN

  • A verified support résumé at the heavy end of bass music: Sullivan King, Stoned Level, Sisto, Finnuh, RZRKT, SYTHYST, Sport Mode and Eradicate — sourced from promoter billing and his own show posts, not a press kit
  • Direct-support reliability: trusted with the slot under Onex & Moros at Almost Human (Connect San Diego) and under Eradicate at Breakpoint PB — the slots where the room has to be warm and the crowd already moving
  • Multiple acts in one booking: solo XEODIN, XEOPHN (b2b DLPHN), b2b Dobie (“Dobie vs XEODIN,” the 1v1 format), and b3b lineups with DLPHN & Dismantlz or DLPHN & Da Impaler
  • A collective behind him: 1/6 of 6th Sense (“Genuine dubstep. No B.S.”), San Diego’s riddim/dubstep crew — booking him plugs into that whole local infrastructure
  • Proven beyond San Diego: headline at Audiobar Las Vegas, LA debut at Oblivion, SF debut with Basswoods, Circle OC, Top Floor DTSA — and a full live set (Almost Human b2b) on SoundCloud to hear before you book
  • He sells the show: ticket links in bio, his own promo code (XOD), contest wins (Bass Jungle) converted into support slots — a promoter-minded DJ

The quick version

XEODIN (Christopher Dakota Gonzalez) is a San Diego dubstep, tearout and riddim DJ/producer — one sixth of the 6th Sense collective — whose 2026 run reads like a speedrun of the West Coast bass circuit: direct support for Onex & Moros at Almost Human, a Sullivan King night at Nova SD, opening for Stoned Level at The Circle OC, a headline set in Las Vegas, debuts in Los Angeles and San Francisco, and a Halloween-night XEOPHN set at Night Fall in San Diego supporting Sisto and Vampa b2b Badvoid. Rave Haven’s billing for him is the shortest accurate review: high energy, overwhelming intensity, constant delivery of chaos.
