Necio — The Finalist Who Came Up Through the Festival Crowd

A festival kid who crossed the barrier — YourShot USA finalist, Latin house in his hands, and a ticket link that is always his own.

KEEPITIL

Most DJs announce a debut. Necio announced his with a date, a venue, a promo code and physical tickets in hand. On October 16, 2025, he posted it plainly: “Making my DJ debut with @yourshot_usa NOV. 15!!! I’ll be performing at Catch ONE in Los Angeles.. 18+” — and then the part that tells you how he works: “DM me for $20 physical tickets — Online Tickets Promo Code: NECIO.” A first gig is usually something that happens to a new DJ. Necio sold his, in person, one DM at a time. By the time he applied to KEEPITIL in June 2026, his Instagram bio carried a title most debut DJs never get: “@yourshot_usa FINALIST.”

Necio — Jovanny Gonzalez, initials J.G., the first line of his own bio — is a Latin House and Tech House DJ from the San Fernando Valley, the “LA•SFV” pin in that same bio. His is a short public record so far, and this article doesn’t pretend otherwise. What the record does show, post by post, is a specific and increasingly rare path: a decade-deep festival fan who studied the circuit from the crowd side of the barrier, then crossed it — finalist first, club bookings after, and a map that already stretches from Los Angeles to Fresno to Salinas.

Raised under the Electric Sky

Before there was a Necio set, there was a Necio in the crowd — and the receipts go back years. His feed is a festival diary long before it is a DJ page. EDC Las Vegas runs through it like a spine: 2022 (“A weekend full of amazing music, people, experiences and emotions!”), 2023 — his third EDC, and the one where he wrote the line that explains the act — “EDC is where my imagination goes wild” — and May 2026, when he marked 30 years of EDC by spending “another year in the desert — Under the Electric Sky,” at his first official Camp EDC and his fifth EDC overall. “Feeling inspired as I continue my music journey,” he wrote that weekend, “and can’t wait to make it to the big stages alongside my family and friends who have supported me in any way, big or small.”

The diary is wider than one festival. Coachella 2024 (“here for the music”). Beyond Wonderland in 2021 and again in 2025. Nocturnal Wonderland 2022, camping and silent discos. Countdown NYE to open 2022. HARD Summer 2025. And, closest to his own sound, Skyline Festival in February 2022, where he posted the thesis of his future sets in all caps: “HOUSE MUSIC ALL DAY LONG” — “house and techno being blasted down the streets of L.A. is the most beautiful thing to happen so far.” The DJs he tagged that day — Chris Lake, Lee Foss, Cloonee, Carl Cox — are a fair map of the Latin house / tech house lane he now plays in.

The debut: a final, not a first gig

YourShot USA bills itself, in its own bio, as “The World’s Most Transformative DJ Competition” — full training provided, no experience necessary. That is the door Necio walked through, and he walked out the far end of it: a finalist, performing at Catch One in Los Angeles on November 15, 2025. His announcement carousel pairs the event poster (“I’M PLAYING,” Saturday Nov 15, 12 PM–late, Catch One Nightclub) with a portrait of him smiling in a green-and-white Mexico jersey, hand over his heart, under a banner reading “YOUR 2025 USA SHOT LOS ANGELES.” The Mexican flag in his bio is not decoration; it is the brand — the same identity his Latin house sets are built on — worn on the flyer of his first-ever show.

The debut also set the working pattern every later booking follows: he promotes what he plays. Physical tickets by DM, a personal promo code online, and a thank-you list that names the people around the competition. Plenty of finalists treat a contest final as a finish line. His next posts treat it as a starting gun.

The circuit since

The bookings that follow the final sketch a working regional circuit, and each one stretches a different muscle. Winter Jamz, Woodley Park (January 2026) — Summer Bopz Radio’s community day party, 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. in the park, billed “…for the children & the animals!!” Necio played an 11 a.m.–12 p.m. back-to-back as NECIO b2b I.M.EARL, alongside Johnny V Jr. Banda Rave, Los Arcos, Fresno (March 20, 2026) — the event posts promise “HEAVY LATIN HOUSE, HEAVY TECHNO BANGERS, BANDA AND NONSTOP VIBES,” with Necio beside DJ Ferny, DJ Aaron and Maznoize, plus live banda from Los Ases de la Banda, at 1435 Fresno St. Pattern Bar, DTLA (June 2026) — when Pattern Bar closed after 15 years, DJ Tensión’s vlog of the last Saturday night billed it “One Last Dance with @thats.necio” and documented an impromptu back-to-back between the two of them. Raveton, Salinas (July 18, 2026) — a boiler-room-format Latin EDM night for Salinas Rodeo weekend at 201 Social Nights. Los Globos Red Room, Los Angeles (August 7, 2026) — “B2B ALERT!!” with fellow KEEPITIL applicant ManyyVibezz, and, in his words, “First time playing this historic venue in Los Angeles.” And his current bio link is his ticket link for the SoundPeak 1 Year Anniversary at Melrose House (November 7, 2026).

Read together, the pattern is range inside a lane: park sets and club sets, a banda-rave hybrid in Fresno, a boiler-room format in Salinas, back-to-back chemistry with at least three different partners, and a Los Angeles room with real history. All of it inside ten months of his debut.

The sound

His application answers the genre question in four words — Latin House / Tech House — and his bio repeats it verbatim: “DJ - Latin House/Tech House.” The event billing fills in the edges without contradicting it: Banda Rave sells his set as heavy Latin house next to heavy techno bangers; Raveton files him under Latin EDM; the Los Globos flyer footage shows him working DJ gear outdoors in a Chris Lake t-shirt, Latin house playing. There are no releases or mixes publicly linked from his profiles that this research could verify, so the honest description of the sound is the one the rooms themselves give: Latin-rooted house and tech house, played by a DJ whose formation was five years of standing in festival crowds listening for exactly those records.

The quick version

Necio (Jovanny Gonzalez) is a San Fernando Valley Latin House / Tech House DJ who came up through the crowd — five EDC Las Vegas editions and a shelf of Insomniac festivals documented on his own feed — before crossing the barrier as a YourShot USA finalist, debuting at Catch One in Los Angeles on November 15, 2025. In the ten months since, he has played a community park party in the Valley, Banda Rave in Fresno, a boiler-room Latin EDM night in Salinas, Pattern Bar’s closing night in DTLA and a back-to-back at Los Globos’ Red Room, and his bio already points at his next date: SoundPeak’s anniversary at Melrose House on November 7, 2026.
