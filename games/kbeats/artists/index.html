KBEATS

For Artists

KBeats turns your music into a playable rhythm track and pays you for the game play it brings in.

How submission works

  • Create your artist profile. Adding your SoundCloud page is optional; KBeats only links to it.
  • Upload a master you control (WAV, FLAC, high-quality MP3 or M4A).
  • KBeats analyses the tempo, beats, drops and sustained sections and builds Easy, Normal and Expert charts. An automatic validator rejects anything physically unplayable.
  • Preview the charts and edit them in the browser editor.
  • Complete the rights attestation and submit for review.

Earnings

You get 90% of the distributable game revenue attributable to your track, and KBeats keeps 10%. Your streaming royalties are not affected. Analytics will cover starts, unique players, completion, accuracy, favorites, follows, SoundCloud click-throughs and region-level counts, with no personal player data.

Beta status

Upload, analysis, chart generation, preview and editing work today in your browser, and you can play your own track straight away on that device. Publishing to every player, moderation and payouts switch on when artist accounts launch.
