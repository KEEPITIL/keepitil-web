## Contest-law question for Game Night (not legal advice)

Paid entry plus a prize is where US contest rules bite. A prize won by chance with a paid entry is a lottery, which is illegal for a private company in most states. A skill contest can use paid entry, but several states restrict or ban paid-entry skill contests or require registration or bonding above a prize value, and rules differ by state and by whether the game involves luck. **Question for a lawyer:** can KEEPITIL charge entry for a bracket in KMOB, KWARS or PokaSnap with cash or merch prizes, and in which states do we exclude entrants? **Safe default until answered:** free entry, prizes funded by sponsors, no cash prizes, an age rule, and written official rules before the first night.
