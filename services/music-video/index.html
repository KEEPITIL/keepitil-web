FAQ

Is this a checkout?

No. You send a request and we reply by email with scope and pricing before any work begins. Nothing is charged on this page.

What do I need to send?

A link to your song, your final owner-approved lyrics, and any images you own or may reuse. We do not transcribe or correct lyrics for you.

How many corrections do I get?

One. For Hook it fixes our mistakes, for Full Lyric Cut it is one consolidated text and timing correction, and for Character Story it is one correction after the storyboard approval. A new concept or style is a separate job.

Is this fully animated?

No. It is motion, beat cuts and captions over still artwork. There is no lip-sync and no live-action filming.

Can you guarantee streams?

No. We do not guarantee streams or reach, and we do not distribute your song.

Why might my Character Story wait?

That option depends on our available capacity. If we are busy, your request is queued, or we use artwork you supply, with your agreement.

What happens to my request?

It arrives as an email to our team, is used only to reply, and is not published.
