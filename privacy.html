Privacy Policy

KEEPITIL · Last updated 2026-07-10
⚖️ Good-faith starting point tailored to KEEPITIL. Have counsel confirm it fits your jurisdictions (e.g., CCPA/GDPR) before or shortly after launch.

This policy explains what KEEPITIL collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account info you provide: email, username/handle, profile details, and content you post.
  • Payment info for purchases: processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation and limited billing details (like the last 4 digits) but never your full card number.
  • Usage analytics: we use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used (pages viewed, general interactions, approximate location). Clarity may record anonymized session interactions to improve UX.
  • Technical data: device/browser type and cookies needed for the site to work.

How we use it

  • To run your account, process payments, and deliver memberships/tickets.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the platform.
  • To send you service messages and, if you opt in, newsletters (you can unsubscribe anytime).

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run KEEPITIL (e.g., Stripe for payments, Supabase for our database, Google/Microsoft for analytics) under their own terms, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Access, update, or delete your profile from your account, or by contacting support.
  • Opt out of analytics via your browser settings / Global Privacy Control where supported.
  • Unsubscribe from newsletters via the link in any email.

Data retention & security

We keep data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is 100% secure.

Children

KEEPITIL is for adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the contact/support link on keepitil.com.
