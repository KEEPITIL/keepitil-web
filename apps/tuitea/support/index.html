TUITEA Support

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Getting help

Email info@keepitil.com. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

You can also report a problem from inside the app, which sends the same thing along with your app version and the screen you were on. It does not send your family records.

Common things

The sign-in link did not work. Request a new one; links expire. Check that you opened it on the same device you asked from.

Apple Health is not showing anything. Check the permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → TUITEA, then reopen the app.

Someone in my household cannot see a record. Records are private unless shared with the household. Open the record and check who it is shared with.

I want my account deleted. Your account → Delete account, in the app. It is immediate and cannot be undone.
