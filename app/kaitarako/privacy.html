Kai Tarako is a free letter-tracing app for young children, made by KEEPITIL. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with information. The short version: a child never signs in and never types anything about themselves. Without a grown-up account, nothing leaves the device. With one, a parent chooses to save their children’s progress to it, after agreeing to what is saved and confirming with a code from their email.

Two ways to use Kai Tarako

  • Without an account (the default): everything below under “What stays on your device” stays on the device. The app makes no network requests.
  • With a grown-up account (optional, in the Grown-ups area): a parent signs in with a code sent to their email (there is no password) and can then turn on saving. Progress is kept safe and follows the child across the parent’s devices.

What stays on your device

So your child can pick up where they left off, the app saves a small amount of information on the device:

  • the settings a grown-up chooses (help level, mode, starting step and similar options);
  • simple progress counts for each lesson: how many times it was finished, how often the “?” help button was tapped, and the date it was last practised;
  • a simple skill record for each letter, word or sound (for example “trace:a”): how many times it was done, how many of those needed help (a second try or tracing help), how many were right first time on their own, and the dates (day only, at most the last 60) on which it was right on their own;
  • the date progress was first saved;
  • if a grown-up adds more than one child: each child’s picture and colour (chosen from a fixed list; there is no place to type a name) and that child’s own progress, as above;
  • the Grown-ups lock end time: a timestamp only, kept on this device;
  • with an account: the grown-up’s sign-in (kept separately from the child’s progress, never in a backup file), the parent’s email so the next code can be sent, and a short list of activity events waiting to be saved (see below).

The app does not save the drawings or finger paths your child makes.

What we collect with a grown-up account

Only after the parent reads and agrees to the consent notice in the app and confirms it with a new code from their email (“verifiable parental consent”). Until then, nothing about a child is sent.

Optional: help make Kai better

Separately, and only if you turn it on (it is off unless you do, with its own code), we may include your children in anonymous totals, such as how many children found a letter on their own. A total is only used when at least 10 children are in it, and it never includes a name, picture, account or any identifier. You can turn this off at any time in Grown-ups.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it for advertising. Your child’s information is never shown to other users. Sharing with a teacher’s class is not available in this version; if it is added later, it will need your separate consent for each class you approve. We use a database service provider to store the account information above on our behalf; it may only use it to provide that service to us. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Nothing for your child to follow: no ads, no tracking

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics companies, no tracking across apps or websites and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party software kits in the app. The only network requests go to KEEPITIL’s own account service, and only when a grown-up account is in use.
  • No in-app purchases, chat, social features or links for your child to follow.
  • Speech is the device’s built-in voices. Nothing is recorded, and no audio is sent anywhere.

Backups

In the Grown-ups area you can Save backup to make a file with the progress and settings above. The file goes wherever you choose to save it on your device. We never receive it. Restore backup reads such a file back in. The app checks the file and ignores anything that isn’t a Kai Tarako backup.

The Grown-ups area (parental gate)

Settings, the account, backups, this policy and anything that leaves the app sit behind a grown-ups gate. To open it, press and hold the Grown-ups button for 3 seconds, then answer a times-table question (for example, 14 × 6). A wrong answer closes the gate, and after two wrong answers it locks for one minute. This keeps young children out; it is not a password and does not identify anyone.

How long we keep information

  • Progress and children’s pictures and colours: until you delete them, or 24 months after your account was last used, when the account is deleted.
  • Activity events: 13 months, then deleted.
  • Consent records: kept after an account is deleted, holding only ids, dates and what was agreed, so we can show consent was handled properly. [retention period: placeholder for the owner and counsel]

Deleting information

  • Stop saving (Grown-ups → Your account → Privacy choices): nothing more is sent; the app keeps working on the device.
  • Delete a child (Grown-ups → Children): removes that child and their progress from the device and from your account.
  • Delete account (Grown-ups → Your account → Privacy choices): removes your account and every child’s saved progress and activity from KEEPITIL. The device keeps its own copy until you delete it.
  • Delete all progress (Grown-ups → Privacy): removes all progress and settings from this device and signs the device out.
  • Removing the app deletes everything it saved on the device. Any backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children’s privacy and parents’ rights

Kai Tarako is made for children 5 and under. Children never create accounts, sign in or enter personal information. A parent or guardian can, at any time:

  • Review what is saved: the progress report and children are in the Grown-ups area. To get a copy of what your account holds, email us.
  • Delete it, as described above, or ask us to by email.
  • Refuse further collection with Stop saving, without losing the use of the app on the device.
  • Change your mind about the optional anonymous totals at any time.

Questions about your child’s information: email info@keepitil.com. We will ask you to confirm the request from your account’s email.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Every table is protected so that a parent can reach only their own children’s information, and nothing about a child can be stored without verified consent. Only a small number of KEEPITIL staff can reach the database, for running and fixing the service.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If a change affects what we collect from children or how we use it, we will ask a parent again in the app before it applies.
