Kai Tarako help and support

Kai Tarako is a free letter-tracing app for young children, made by KEEPITIL LLC. It has no ads and no in-app purchases. Children never sign in; a grown-up can choose to save progress to an account.

Contact us

Email info@keepitil.com. A grown-up should write to us; please don’t include your child’s name or other details about them.

Common questions

How do I open the Grown-ups area? Press and hold the Grown-ups button for 3 seconds, then answer the times-table question. With VoiceOver, Switch Control or a keyboard, activating the button goes straight to the question.

How do I move progress to a new device? Either sign in to the same grown-up account on both devices (Grown-ups → Save progress to your account), or use Save backup on the old device and Restore backup on the new one.

How do I delete everything? On a device: Grown-ups → Privacy → Delete all progress, or delete the app. In your account: Grown-ups → Your account → Privacy choices → Delete account. You can also email us and we’ll delete it for you.
