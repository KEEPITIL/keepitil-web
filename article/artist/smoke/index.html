Smoke — The DJ Who Builds His Own Rooms

Plenty of DJs wait for the phone to ring. Smoke — founder of Savage Entertainment — built the phone.

KEEPITIL

On October 2, 2026, in a DTLA warehouse running four dance floors and more than 47 DJs, Smoke played support for Gene Farris — the Chicago house legend whose name anchors the top of the bill at Putivuelta + Groove-ism + Refuze’s mini-festival. Smoke wasn’t just on that bill; he was selling it. His Instagram bio carried the ticket link with his own promo code — SMOKENTRAXX — the mark of a DJ who moves tickets, not just decks. For an Orange County house DJ, a support slot under Gene Farris is a career line. For Smoke, it’s almost a side hustle: the rest of the time, he’s the one building the room.

That’s the thing the résumé keeps proving. Smoke — Daniel Herrera, of Santa Ana — is a DJ/producer who also founded Savage Entertainment, the OC events company behind a stack of the county’s underground nights. Plenty of DJs wait for the phone to ring. Smoke built the phone.

Founder first

His Instagram bio says it in four words: “Founder @savage.ent420.” Savage Entertainment — Savage ENT — describes its mission as “bringing people together through electrifying events, creating a vibrant community where music, energy, and connection come to life,” and its home base is Top Floor, the downtown Santa Ana room at 315 N Main St whose handle sits in the company’s bio. The Savage catalogue is a working promoter’s catalogue: the Hypnotic one-year anniversary in January 2025 — two stages of house and techno headlined by Diskreet, with Smoke himself on the bill and Savage ENT hosting; Riddim Rapture in June 2025, a night of riddim at Top Floor; Subby Wubby, a dubstep night co-hosted with Animal Styl Events; a “Savage room” hosting Miami talent at a Rollerz Club takeover. In March 2026, Savage ENT co-presented SPLIT SIGNAL with 6H0ST Recordings — “No genres. No boundaries. Just sound.” — at Top Floor, with open decks from 9 p.m. for anyone ready to step up.

That open-decks detail matters. Smoke’s rooms aren’t closed shops; they’re infrastructure other DJs climb on. And his company is genre-agnostic in a way his own sets mirror: the same promoter who books riddim and dubstep nights DJs house and techno.

The sound

Ask the application and the answer is plain: house, tech house and techno. The wider record agrees and adds shading — KEEPITIL’s own directory tags him “House / Minimal / Techno / Afro / Latin,” and the rooms fill in the rest. At Verde in Costa Mesa, the Microdose night he played in March 2026 was billed, in all caps, “MINIMAL DEEP TECH HOUSE.” His warehouse afters reels promise “HOUSE AND TECHNO ALL NIGHT LONG” — 11 p.m. to 5 a.m. And then there’s the range nobody can fake: in August 2026, at Mission Bar in Santa Ana, DJ Smoke spun rock en español and ’80s all night next to a live band, Hijos de Don Ramon, for a $5 door — an open-format left turn that a one-genre DJ doesn’t get booked for twice.

The duo work shows the funkier end of the spectrum. SMOKE’N’TRAXXX — Smoke and Jacob Pagoaga, aka TRAXXX — is his standing back-to-back project, and his own pitch for it is pure confidence: at The Compound LA with Plur Eventz, the pair ran “the grooviest most funky set you ever heard,” with “giveaways, tattoos, vendors, and most important you. See you on the dance floor.” The duo also has live tape — a b2b set broadcast from Future Studios OC — and the SMOKENTRAXX name doubles as his ticket promo code, brand and act in one word.

The circuit

Smoke’s map is the SoCal underground’s working circuit, and he’s been on it for years. The earliest receipts in his feed are 2023: a main-stage slot at Circle OC for MERGE’s house & techno night — billed, in that era, as “420 Savage” — and thank-you posts to Heat Ultra Lounge OC and Mayhem Events (“I want to thank all of you for showing up to my shows — I hope you have as much fun dancing as I have playing tunes for y’all”). Earlier still, a 2022 set at Catch One in Los Angeles and a NOS Events Center night where the crowd stayed till the end.

The current circuit is denser. Proof Bar in Santa Ana is a recurring room — in July 2026 he played the TMZEE takeover presented by 6HOST Recordings LLC, on a bill with fellow KEEPITIL applicant Richrunthat; days earlier, 6HOST’s foundation celebration at the same bar name-checked “Darce, TMZEE, Smoke N Traxxx, Manuel Silva” as part of the label’s journey. The Continental Room in downtown Fullerton earned his loudest review: “THE CONTINENTAL ROOM LIVED UP TO THE HYPE!! WE CAN’T WAIT TO BE BACK!” In May 2026 he closed the Huntington Beach House day party — his own announcement: “I will be closing up the event from 6pm to 7pm and do I got a fun set for y’all” — the closing slot, again, being the trust slot. And with Microdose, the OC warehouse-afters brand (“A DOSE OF HOUSE MUSIC — OC”), his reels document the 11-to-5 a.m. warehouse run that is the underground’s real economy.

Then there are the formats that show the showman. In September 2026, Smoke went head-to-head with Mr. Sir in Moo Talk’s Face-to-Face DJ Battle — “Two DJs. Face-to-face. No hiding behind the decks” — hosted by Kaytexs and livestreamed. In October he was on the bill for Enter the Cage in DTLA, a Jersey club, footwork and bass night — one more genre room he can walk into. Battle DJ, afters DJ, day-party closer, rock en español opener: the bookings keep asking different questions, and he keeps answering them.

The work behind the decks

The “producer” half of his DJ/producer billing is documented, if modestly publicized. His SoundCloud — the first link in his bio — carries his mixes; a 2023 reel ribs his own followers about it: “Some of y’all never even heard my mixes lol.” His story highlights announce an original, “Liquid Jungle,” out on SoundCloud with a music video teased. Add the promoter’s workload — Savage ENT’s own calendar, the duo, the battle and support slots — and Smoke reads less like a DJ looking for a break than like a small company with a resident DJ who happens to be its founder.

He is also, not incidentally, already in the KEEPITIL pipeline the honest way: he applied to the platform’s DJ intake twice (April and June 2026) — a repeat applicant, like Queue #1 RAVE GOD, whose 6HOST orbit he shares. The scene he’s part of is already applying. This feature is the paperwork catching up.

Why book Smoke

  • A founder’s draw: he runs Savage Entertainment (Top Floor DTSA’s orbit) and sells tickets with his own promo code — he played support for Gene Farris (Oct 2, 2026, DTLA) on a 47-DJ, four-floor bill, and he promotes what he plays
  • Slot-proof: day-party closer (Huntington Beach House), warehouse afters (Microdose, 11p–5a), main stage (Circle OC), support for a global headliner, and a face-to-face battle format (Moo Talk vs Mr. Sir, livestreamed)
  • Two acts in one booking: solo Smoke, or SMOKE’N’TRAXXX, his b2b with Jacob Pagoaga (live tape from Future Studios OC)
  • Range beyond the underground: house / tech house / techno / minimal at the core, with documented open-format work (rock en español & ’80s night at Mission Bar, Santa Ana)
  • Promoter infrastructure, not just a guest: his own events brand, a label circle (6HOST Recordings), and rooms across OC, LA and the IE — his bio’s territory line is “OC•LA•IE” and the receipts back it

The quick version

Smoke (Daniel Herrera) is a Santa Ana house, tech house and techno DJ/producer who founded Savage Entertainment — the events company behind Hypnotic’s anniversary, Riddim Rapture and SPLIT SIGNAL at Top Floor DTSA — and plays the circuit he helps build: Proof Bar, the Continental Room, Circle OC, Microdose’s warehouse afters, a closing set at Huntington Beach House, a face-to-face DJ battle, and an October 2026 support slot for Gene Farris in DTLA. Half of the duo SMOKE’N’TRAXXX, with mixes and an original (“Liquid Jungle”) on SoundCloud, he is the rare booking who is also the infrastructure.
