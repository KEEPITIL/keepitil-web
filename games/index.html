📱 Install on your phone (free, no app store)

  1. Open the app on your phone and tap Play Now.
  2. iPhone / iPad: tap the Share button ⬆ in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Android: tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Launch it from your home screen — it runs full-screen like a native app, even offline.
