KEEPITIL PRESENTS

OSCARÍN — From the Sand to the Skyline

A beach in Huntington Beach one summer, a rooftop above Broadway the next. OSCARÍN is building his name one honest bill at a time.

KEEPITIL

The two rooms that define OSCARÍN so far could not be further apart, and he has played them both. In July 2025 he was on the sand in Huntington Beach, billed under his own name — OSCAR MARIN — on KEEPITIL's summer beach-party bill. In September 2026 he was on a rooftop above Broadway in downtown Los Angeles, on the HOUSE OF TEK lineup at Somewhere Special LA, throwing down tech house on a Thursday night. Beach to skyline, Whittier to DTLA: OSCARÍN — Oscar Marin — is a house DJ building his name one honest bill at a time.

His Instagram bio is the whole business card: “DJ🪩 / Tech House/ Latin House/Techno.” No management, no cosigns, no inflated claims — just the disco ball and the genres. The application he filed with KEEPITIL in June 2026 widens the list slightly — Tech House, House, Latin House and techno — and his posts widen it further still. It is a small public footprint, four posts deep. But every post on it is a receipt.

The sound

Start with his own framing. His April 8, 2026 post asks the room directly: “Ready to get groovy with some Tech House & Techno? Let's Run It 🕺🪩” — and the hashtags underneath stretch the brief wider than the bio does: #house #techouse #minimal #techno #reggaeton. Groove is the headline word, and the tag list is a Whittier DJ's honest map of what a Southern California floor actually asks for across a night: house and tech house at the centre, minimal and techno when the room wants it darker, Latin house in the application genres, and reggaeton in the tags for when the party turns all the way up.

The hardest evidence of the range is the reel his own followers responded to most. On August 29, 2026, he posted a “Hard Techno Transition😤🙌🏻🫵🏼” — #hardtechno #filthy #drop #transition — filmed at RATE STUDIOS in Los Angeles: OSCARÍN behind professional Pioneer gear in a dark jersey, hands moving across knobs, faders and jog wheels under blue LED light, cutting into a drop with the confidence of a DJ showing his work on purpose. A transition reel is a craftsman's post. Anybody can screenshot a flyer; a transition is the part you can't fake.

The rooms

The beach came first. The KEEPITIL SUMMER 2025 flyer — Saturday, July 26, 2025, Huntington Beach, 8 p.m. to 2 a.m., tickets through Posh and Eventbrite — bills OSCAR MARIN on a lineup that reads like a roll call of this platform's own scene: LYK N KEY, BANHU, SMOKE, NEGIO, CEASAR GARDEA, HYPE DVTE, ASHLEY CORDELIA, DJ TENSIÓN, ONEXSHOT, RAVE GOD and RICHRUNTHAT. KEEPITIL's own account later carried the party to his profile with the pitch intact: an all-ages Huntington Beach party, sixteen DJs, “EDM, hip hop, and afrobeats right on the sand.” For a Whittier house DJ, a named slot on a bill that size — alongside artists this series has already profiled — is a foundation stone, not a footnote.

The rooftop is the current headline. On Monday, September 21, 2026, he posted the flyer for HOUSE OF TEK — presented by TEKNLGY X URFRNDS, who bill themselves on the flyer as “THE HOUSE MUSIC NETWORK OF LA” — set for Thursday, September 24, 2026, 9 p.m., at Somewhere Special LA, the rooftop at 929 S Broadway in downtown Los Angeles, 21+. The lineup: URFRNDS, DINO QUACK, ULTRA BEATS, MNDZ, OSCARIN and WETONES. His caption sells it the way a working DJ should: “Throwing down some tech house this Thursday! Slide through and enjoy the vibes and some good music🙌🏻 RSVP link in my bio 🔥” — with the hashtags #techhousemusic #DTLA #rooftop doing the targeting. That is a named slot, on a named rooftop, in the middle of DTLA's Broadway corridor, for a presenter that calls itself the house music network of LA.

Put the two rooms together and a shape emerges: this is a DJ being booked by scenes, not by algorithm. A beach-party bill in Orange County, a studio session in Los Angeles, a rooftop night in DTLA — the posts are few because the work is the point.
