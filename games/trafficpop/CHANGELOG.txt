TRAFFIC POP — changelog (newest first)

2026-10-22  release 4: terminal flow
- Boarding now follows the whole route: the vehicle pulls out of its bay (the space frees), drives to the
  highlighted loading dock, passengers walk aboard one by one, and it drives away
- Exit lanes are marked around every edge of the lot; taxis join the traffic (they behave like cars)
- A blocked tap now shows the road the vehicle wanted and the vehicle in its way
- New engine and boarding sounds, star chimes and a fuller win fanfare (all generated, no audio files)
- The level you are playing is saved after every move: reload or come back later and CONTINUE where you were
- Settings → Playtest data: attempts, wins, times and helper use per level, stored only on this device

2026-10-21  release 3: new journey
- 60 rebuilt levels: packed traffic jams (most vehicles start blocked), 9 to 30+ vehicles per level
- Planned difficulty: it climbs across the journey, every 5th level is a HARD level, and the level after is a breather
- Every colour has spare seats, so one vehicle leaving part-empty is no longer an instant dead end
- Stars now reward smart parking: 3 stars for using few parking spaces; undo is free and never costs stars
- Helpers: +1 Space (an extra parking bay) and Lift (pick up any vehicle, even a blocked one). 3 of each free;
  earn more with first-time 3-star clears and HARD level clears. Using a helper or hint caps that win at 2 stars
- Your unlocked levels and stars carry over (a copy of your old save is kept on this device)

2026-10-21  release 2: new look
- One connected traffic-terminal scene: two colour-coded loading docks, six parking bays, the jam below (beside it in landscape)
- Toy-style vehicles with windows, wheels, seat dots and a large colour symbol; real passenger figures
- Full passenger forecast for each dock
- Animated turns: vehicles drive out of the jam into a bay, passengers hop aboard one by one, full vehicles depart
- Blocked taps show which vehicle is in the way; parking warns when it is nearly full; combo pop-ups
- Hints are worked out in the background and never freeze the game
- Landscape layout on phones, tablets and desktops; settings for sound, vibration, fast animations and reduced motion
- Tap again during an animation to skip it; same rules, same 60 levels, your progress carries over

2026-10-18  first public web release
- 60 Journey levels, each proven solvable by the solver under the real parking and dock rules
- Tap vehicles whose arrow path is clear; 6 parking slots; two passenger docks with NOW / NEXT forecasts
- Hint, Undo, Restart, 3-star results, level map, progress saved on this device
- Colour + shape markers for colour-blind players; reduced-motion support; sound + vibration
