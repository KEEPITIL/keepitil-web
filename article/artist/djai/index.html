DjAi — Three Genres, One Signature: THT

Trance, house and techno — all subgenres — signed with three letters. The Irvine founder of THT Radio is taking them to a Christopher Lawrence support slot.

KEEPITIL

Plenty of DJs put the genres they play in their bio. DjAi put his in an acronym and then built a radio station around it. THT — Techno, House, Trance — is how the Irvine DJ signs his sets (“Caught me mixing some THT… Techno, House, & Trance”), the name of the radio show and recordings project he founded, and, as of his current Instagram bio, the sound he is taking into the biggest support slot on his calendar: “Events: 12/11 — Christopher Lawrence Support.” A post shared across his own page and THT Radio’s put the same energy in four words: “See you all in December!”

Three letters, one promise

The bio does the whole pitch in one line: “Book me for #trance #house #techno.” Underneath it sit two flags — Swiss and Italian, the heritage of Anthony Italia, the man behind the name — and one job description he gave himself: “Founder, DJ & Curator of @tht.radio.”

That founder title is the centre of the DjAi story. He isn’t only a guest on other people’s bills; he curates a platform of his own. THT Radio’s own page bills “THT Radio + THT Recordings” with “Exclusive Sets • Guest Mixes • Original Releases,” names @_djai._ as its owner, and signs off with the same three genres. By early 2026 the show was deep into its run — “THT Radio Episode 55 features a special guest mix from fast growing trance artist @vangarmusic,” he wrote, noting her multiple appearances at Dreamstate and Captured Festival and a new track dropping March 2nd. Episode 55 is not a starting point; it’s a catalogue, and DjAi is its curator.

The circuit he actually plays

DjAi’s feed is a working SoCal trance circuit, documented set by set. One of the earliest receipts is also one of the biggest: in 2023 he played a b2b set with Al3rro — a DJ whose own billing in the event post credits “over 20 years in DJ career” — on a bill with LA trance legend Kristina Sky, a moment he called “one of the biggest and best moments to start off my potential DJ career,” thanking Unroasted Music for the stage. Days earlier in that run, his one-line caption under a photo of himself cueing said what the whole career since has backed up: “The passion I have being behind the decks keeps on growing.”

From there the rooms stack up. Techno at SoundTech OC (“Had a blast spinning techno… Can’t wait to be back behind the decks somewhere in the world bringing you more techno!”). Techno at the Mob Bar in Pomona, “ready to bring more.” A Halloween 2024 bill — “Trick or Trance?” — alongside Mizz0cheese and DJ Aaron Funk. A November 21, 2024 slot on Darkroom Events’ Dreamstate pre-party, “performing alongside a family of DJs, producers, and artists.” An opening set on “a night of house & techno in Santa Ana… Bringing my unique THT style set.” A throwback photo with Victor Ruiz, posted with the Tranceformity group tagged. And in December 2025, a resident’s spot at Brooks Bar in Riverside, where Tranceformity and Epic Sounds closed out the year with “our residents @technician_86 and @_djai._” on the bill.

Then there’s the hometown thread KEEPITIL readers will recognise. In the summer of 2024 he played the iL Summer Music Festival b2b with Garfelli — “Super excited to be a part of @keepitil and playing at il Summer music festival!” — and called it the “Highlight of the year!” The connection didn’t end there: in July 2026 he was promoting KEEPITIL Summer at Magnolia & CA-1, a beach-day bill he shared with Bakhubass and Summer Bopz Radio. The Irvine DJ and the Garden Grove platform keep crossing paths — which is, in the end, how he got here: he applied.

The nights he helps build

The curator side shows up in the bills, too. In 2026, Tranceformity Group teamed up with THT Recordings — his recordings arm — for nights in Corona at Rockafellas, with Epic Sounds building “a fully immersive sound and lighting experience” and lineups billed as “a multi genre journey, featuring high energy selections across trance, house, and dubstep.” He also played support at a Vangar release party in 2026, “grateful to be part of this release party and to support @vangarmusic on a day that’s all about TRANCEformation through sound.” Support slots, residency slots, co-presented nights: the pattern is a DJ who is inside the production, not just on the poster.

The producer: “Sleep Awake”

The DJing came first — “Deejaying is the beginning for me. The producing is coming soon,” he posted early on — and then, in 2025, the first finished original arrived. “After almost 2 years of learning electronic music production, starting and not finishing many projects, I finally finished this one!” he wrote. “Sleep Awake is exactly what the title entails, no sleep and constantly trying to stay awake to finish this track.” He thanked Eric P for teaching him Ableton, Vangar for inspiring him, and BMO among the friends and strangers whose feedback he chased. It’s an honest producer’s origin story — two years of unfinished projects and one finished track that kept him up at night, named for exactly that.

The recorded side runs through his link-in-bio page, titled “Sounds and Sets of Ai,” and his SoundCloud, where the mix from his Sensory Sessions radio appearance lives: “I am really excited to share this and also have the opportunity to be a part of a radio show! … If you missed the live stream, no worries, the mix is posted up on my SoundCloud!”

The sound, in his own words

Ask what holds techno, house and trance together in one set and his captions answer it. The genre he returns to, emotionally, is trance: “Trance — I love it. I love spinning it. Thank you @_darkroom_events & @tranceformity for having me!” The other two are the tools that let a trance DJ play any room on the poster — a techno room in Pomona, a house-and-techno opener in Santa Ana, a hypertechno and uplifting trance bill in Corona. The application he filed says the same thing in paperwork form: Trance, House, Techno — all subgenres.

Why book DjAi

  • A headline support slot on the books: his bio lists a December 11 Christopher Lawrence support date — direct support for one of trance’s defining names
  • Three genres, genuinely played: trance, house and techno (all subgenres, per his application), with documented sets in each — techno rooms, house openers and trance bills
  • A platform, not just a set: founder, DJ and curator of THT Radio and THT Recordings — 55+ radio episodes in, with guest mixes from rising trance artists, plus co-presented nights with Tranceformity Group
  • Proven b2b chemistry: back-to-back sets with Al3rro (on a Kristina Sky bill) and with Garfelli at the iL Summer Music Festival
  • An original in the catalogue: “Sleep Awake,” his first finished production after two years of learning the craft, alongside mixes on SoundCloud
  • Scene-loyal and local: Irvine-based, playing OC, Riverside and Corona rooms, residencies and pre-parties across the SoCal underground — and a repeat face at KEEPITIL’s own summer events

The quick version

DjAi (Anthony Italia) is an Irvine DJ who plays trance, house and techno — all subgenres — and signs the combination THT: Techno, House, Trance. He is the founder, DJ and curator of THT Radio and THT Recordings, which passed Episode 55 in 2026, and a working fixture of the SoCal trance circuit: a b2b set on a Kristina Sky bill, a Dreamstate pre-party, a Brooks Bar residency night, co-presented Tranceformity × THT Recordings shows in Corona, festival sets for KEEPITIL, and his first finished original, “Sleep Awake.” His bio currently lists the biggest line yet — December 11, supporting Christopher Lawrence.
