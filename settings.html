Delete your account?

Your profile, profile page, saved events, follows and settings are deleted. Community posts and replies that other people answered stay, with your name and words removed. Events you submitted are not removed automatically: email info@keepitil.com before you delete your account if you want them taken down. Ticket orders you placed are kept for our records. This cannot be undone.
