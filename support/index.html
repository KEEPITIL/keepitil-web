Frequently asked questions

I can't sign in

Use the password-reset option in the app (for TUITEA: Forgot password) and follow the link we email you. Links expire, so request a new one if it does not work. Still stuck? Send a request above with the email address you sign in with.

How do I get a refund or cancel a subscription?

Purchases made inside an iPhone app are handled by Apple: cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. For purchases made on keepitil.com, see our Refund Policy. In TUITEA, Restore purchases brings back a plan you already pay for.

Something isn't working

Close and reopen the app, check your connection, then send a request above with the app and what you were doing. A screenshot helps — email it to info@keepitil.com with your reference number.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Most apps have Delete account in their account or settings screen. For TUITEA: Your account → Delete account (you confirm with your password). You can also send a privacy request above.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes — choose Feedback or a feature request above. Every request is read by the KEEPITIL team.
