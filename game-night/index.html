Entry is free, and watching is always free. We announce each night here, with the game, prizes and rules, before sign-up opens.

How it works

  1. Join the list by email and we send you the details when a night is announced.
  2. Play your matches on the night. A host calls each round.
  3. Finals are streamed. Winners are posted here, with their consent.

Prizes and sponsors

Prizes are provided by sponsors. Prizes are never cash: they are merchandise, gift items or credit in kind. Each night's prize list is published here before sign-up opens.

Want to sponsor a night? Email us and we will send the options.

Official rules (version 1, November 2026)

  1. Free to enter. No purchase or payment is needed to enter, play or win. Nothing you buy changes your chances.
  2. Who can play. You must be 13 or older. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to your entry. One entry per person per night. KEEPITIL staff and the night's sponsors can take part but cannot win prizes.
  3. How winners are decided. Winners are decided only by results in the game, through the bracket. Nothing is decided by chance or by a drawing. The host settles ties and disputes, and the host's decision is final.
  4. Prizes. Prizes are non-cash, provided by sponsors, and listed with their approximate value before each night. There is no cash alternative. Prizes cannot be transferred. If a prize is unavailable, we may replace it with one of similar value.
  5. Fair play. No cheating, exploits, collusion, multiple accounts or harassment. We follow the Community Guidelines. The host can remove a player from a night for breaking these rules.
  6. Your information. We use your name and email only to run the night and to contact winners. We name winners publicly only with their consent.
  7. Changes. We may change, postpone or cancel a night, and we will say so here. Void where prohibited by law.
  8. Questions. Email info@keepitil.com.
