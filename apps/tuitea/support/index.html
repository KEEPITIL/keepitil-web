TUITEA Support

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Getting help

Open the KEEPITIL help desk for TUITEA: ask a question or send a message to the team. This is the main place to get help.

Email info@keepitil.com. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

You can also report a problem from inside the app, which sends the same thing along with your app version and the screen you were on. It does not send your family records.

Common things

I cannot sign in. TUITEA signs in with your email address and a password. If you have never set one, or have forgotten it, tap Forgot password in the app and use the link we email you. Links expire, so request a new one if it does not work.

Apple Health is not showing anything. Check the permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → TUITEA, then reopen the app.

Someone in my household cannot see a record. Records are private unless shared with the household. Open the record and check who it is shared with.

I want my account deleted. Your account → Delete account, in the app. You confirm with your password; deletion is then immediate and cannot be undone.
