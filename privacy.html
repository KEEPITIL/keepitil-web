Privacy Policy

KEEPITIL · Last updated November 27, 2026
⚖️ Good-faith starting point tailored to KEEPITIL. Have counsel confirm it fits your jurisdictions (e.g., CCPA/GDPR) before or shortly after launch.

This policy explains what KEEPITIL collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account info you provide: your email address (you sign in with it, with a password or a one-time link, or through Google or Apple; if you use Google or Apple they share your name and email address with us), a username/handle, profile details, and content you post.
  • Event RSVPs: when you tap "I'm going" on an event page we store your RSVP with a random device ID kept in your browser (so the count is right and you are not counted twice) and, only if you choose to add one, your email address so we can send a reminder. You do not need an account to RSVP.
  • Payment info when you buy a ticket, enter a competition or buy an artist service: processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation and limited billing details (like the last 4 digits) but never your full card number.
  • Usage analytics: so we can see how the site is used, pages load Google Analytics 4 (a Google service: which pages are viewed, general interactions, approximate location) and, on computers, Microsoft Clarity (a Microsoft service that can record anonymized clicks, scrolling and session interactions; it also talks to Microsoft's Bing servers). These providers receive your IP address and device details when they load.
  • Videos: the Culture page can play videos from YouTube (Google) using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube receives your IP address and device details when a video player or its preview picture loads.
  • Technical data: device/browser type and cookies needed for the site to work. KEEPITIL is served through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address when a page loads, and we use IP addresses to limit how often one address can submit our forms, which blocks spam and abuse.

How we use it

  • To run your account, process payments, and deliver tickets and competition entries.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the platform.
  • To send you service messages and, if you opt in, newsletters (you can unsubscribe anytime).

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run KEEPITIL (e.g., Stripe for payments, Supabase for our database, Cloudflare for hosting and security, Google and Apple if you choose to sign in with them, Google/Microsoft for analytics) under their own terms, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Access, update, or delete your profile from your account, or by contacting support.
  • Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are honored. If your browser sends either one, we do not load Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity, and a Culture video only starts after you tap play. (Video preview pictures still come from Google's image servers.)
  • Unsubscribe from newsletters via the link in any email.

Data retention & security

We keep data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is 100% secure.

Children

KEEPITIL is for adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the contact/support link on keepitil.com.
