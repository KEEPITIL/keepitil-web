KEEPITIL PRESENTS

ManyyVibezz — Manyy More Vibezz Where That Came From

A Reseda raver who practised in public — a mix a day, a single after single — until the crowd years turned into his own releases and sets.

KEEPITIL

The slogan is his own, and he puts it everywhere: in his Instagram bio, under the photos of his first set of 2026, at the end of his reels. “There are Manyy More Vibezz Where That Came From!!” For a DJ, that’s a promise about volume — and ManyyVibezz, the Reseda tech house producer born Emmanuel Benitez, keeps it the unglamorous way: a mix a day, a single after single, and a 2025 that he himself summed up as the year he went “from djing shows with all my best friends to making and releasing my own music.”

He is the most recent applicant in the KEEPITIL DJ queue — he applied on July 10, 2026 — and in some ways the most representative one: a San Fernando Valley raver who spent years in the crowd at EDC, Beyond Wonderland and Dreamstate, then picked up the craft in public, on camera, one reel at a time, until the reels turned into releases and the releases turned into sets. This is what the on-ramp actually looks like.

From the crowd to the decks

His feed tells the before picture honestly, because it predates the DJ push. EDC 2024: “Created Core memories that will never fade with some really special people in my life!!!! love you guys 🙏 EDC2024 thank you for the immaculate vibes✨🔥🙌 till next year!” Beyond Wonderland 2024, with a shout-out to “all these amazing beautiful people” who brought the vibes. Dreamstate in November 2024: “Once again DreamState did not disappoint!” A December 2023 post from a John Summit show — “Good vibes all around with the squad!!!” Years of showing up as a fan, in the genres he now plays: house, techno, trance’s big-room cousin Dreamstate, and the whole Insomniac calendar.

The pivot is dated by his own year-end post. On December 31, 2025, closing the year, he wrote: “Shout out to 2025 for teaching me that there’s more than just listening to music, from djing shows with all my best friends to making and releasing my own music. This year really was an amazing year that I will never forget!!” That sentence — from listening, to DJing shows, to releasing his own music, all inside one year — is the biography. Everything after it in the feed is the working-out of those three steps.

A mix a day: the public practice room

What ManyyVibezz did differently is practice in public. Through the second half of 2025 and into 2026, his reels are a near-daily mixing diary, hashtagged #manyyvibezz, #techhouse and — constantly — #818, the Valley area code his Reseda base sits in. In July 2025: “At this point a mix a day keeps the doctors away.” “Always lost in the mix.” October 2025: “DJing is fun but I always seem to be lost in the mix!! It really is all about the art of never giving up & having a good time no matter what!!” January 2026, twice in two days, as if to set the year’s terms: “When I say I can’t stop mixing I mean it” and “Its not just playing music is mixing music.”

The diary has range notes a booker can check. In November 2025 he mixed two of his favourite set staples together — Matroda’s “Bullshit” and Desamor’s “I’m On It” — and said so: “These 2 tracks have been on my top favorite songs to play at every set!! I had to mix them.” The same month: “Why not mix a bit of everything.” In October 2025 he flagged the genre spread himself: “It’s ManyyVibezz for a reason!!! One things forsure there’s Manyy more Vibezz where that came from! it’s not always tech house sometimes it’s a bit of everything,” tagging that one #basshouse and #dubstep. And in August 2025, the producer’s tell: “I had to remix my song — Going Crazy - ManyyVibezz X Mind Games - Discip,” folding his own track into someone else’s.

That last habit is the tell for the whole project. The application lists four genres — tech house, house, Afrobeats and techno — and his bio lists tech house, Latin house, techno and Afro house. Put the two lists together and the picture is consistent: a house DJ whose centre of gravity is tech house, whose edges run to Afro and Latin sounds, and who treats other people’s records and his own as one continuous set.

The catalogue: singles, stacked

The releases are where the practice becomes a record, and they are verifiable on his Spotify and Apple Music artist pages, both linked from his Instagram bio. Spotify’s popular list for ManyyVibezz runs: “Getting Out of Hand,” “Welcome to My Bass,” “Downtown,” “Own This” and “This Sound.” The 2026 singles, per the same page: “This Sound” (his latest release), “Welcome to My Bass,” “Glue” and “Own This.” The 2025 singles behind them: “Your Running Out of Time,” “In The Back,” “Forget The Rest,” “Divine” and “Cash in Black Out.”

His own announcements fill in the intent. January 2026: “‘Welcome to My Bass’ — Out this Friday!!” July 2026, over a reel: “My New Track ‘DOWNTOWN’ will be officially out on all platforms tomorrow!!!” A Reseda producer with a 2025–2026 run of self-released singles, a SoundCloud page carrying his sets (the bio link titled “My Sets” sits right under the main SoundCloud link), and Apple Music and Spotify pages under the same name — the infrastructure of an independent release artist, built without a label in sight.

The sets so far

The first documented set of 2026 is a good one to have on the record. In mid-January he posted the photos from a set with the Tech Dogs, shouting out Summer Bopz Radio and Leo Villalobos — himself a fellow KEEPITIL applicant, DJ Tensión, Queue #17 — “for allowing us to vibe with all you guys!!” The caption signs off with the slogan and one piece of crew colour no writer would dare invent: “& of course Chapo is a certified Tech Dog.” (Chapo, for the record, appears to be the dog.)

Since then, his feed has carried the rooms around him: a July 2026 countdown reel for GALAXY, Plur Eventz’s cosmic warehouse rave at The Compound LA — house, techno and hard techno, 8 p.m. to 2 a.m. — with tickets through the link in his bio; an April 2026 ILLUMINATION glow rave at The Compound by Dirt Dog in Los Angeles, a full-concert-stage bill he shared to his feed; and a July 2026 collaborative post with @keepitil itself promoting a Huntington State Beach gathering under the line “We strive to Grow Together to Glow Together.” A new DJ’s early map is always partly other people’s rooms and community bills he’s helping push. His is documented, dated, and all within the Los Angeles scene he’s applying from.

  • Genre coverage inside one booking: tech house and house at the core (his application), with Afrobeats on the application list and Latin house / Afro house on his own bio list, plus documented bass house and dubstep mixing when the room wants it heavier
  • A visible work rate: a public, near-daily mixing diary through 2025–2026 — “a mix a day keeps the doctors away” — and sets on his SoundCloud (the “My Sets” link in his bio) instead of a promise that he practises
  • Scene-native, not scene-adjacent: years in the crowd at EDC, Beyond Wonderland and Dreamstate before his first sets, a January 2026 Tech Dogs set with Summer Bopz Radio, and community bills (Plur Eventz at The Compound LA) already moving through his channels
  • Valley-based and available: a Reseda (818) DJ/producer, the newest applicant in the KEEPITIL queue, with bookings contacts on both his application and his Instagram bio and tickets already sold through his own bio link

The quick version

ManyyVibezz (Emmanuel Benitez) is a Reseda, California DJ/producer working tech house, house, Afrobeats and techno — Latin house and Afro house on his own bio’s list. After years as a fan at EDC, Beyond Wonderland and Dreamstate, he spent 2025 learning in public — a near-daily run of mixing reels tagged #818 — while releasing his own music; his Spotify and Apple Music pages carry a 2025–2026 run of singles including “Downtown,” “Welcome to My Bass,” “This Sound” and “Getting Out of Hand.” His 2026 opened with a Tech Dogs set for Summer Bopz Radio, and his slogan is also his work plan: there are Manyy more Vibezz where that came from.
