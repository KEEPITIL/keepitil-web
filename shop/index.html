FAQ

How do I book my session?

After you check out, we email you within 24 hours to schedule the 45-minute 1:1 remote video call. You can reschedule free with 24 hours' notice.

When do I get my download?

The Release Rollout Kit download link is in your order email, right after checkout.

What do you need for graphics?

After checkout we email you for a short brief: cover art you own, the release title, and the release date. The graphics are delivered within 3 business days of receiving that brief. The listing includes one round of revisions.

What is the refund policy?

Refunds follow the store refund policy. For the Release Strategy Session, you can reschedule free with 24 hours' notice, and the listing says you get a full refund if we can't find a time that works for you.

What does shipping cost, and how long does it take?

Digital items have no shipping. Merch ships at $6.69 flat. The shipping time is the estimate shown at checkout.

What sizes are available?

The KEEPITIL logo tee is listed in XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL, 4XL, and 5XL. Other merch uses the sizes shown on its product page.

Who is KEEPITIL?

KEEPITIL is a culture platform built to discover, connect, create, share and earn through people, events, media and community.

How do I contact KEEPITIL?

Email info@keepitil.com.
