Community Guidelines

KEEPITIL · Last updated 2026-07-11

KEEPITIL is a home for the people who make culture — creators, organizers, brands, and the communities around them. These guidelines keep it safe, real, and welcoming. They apply everywhere on KEEPITIL: profiles, posts, community rooms, events, and the culture blog.

Respect the scene & each other

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or hate speech targeting anyone based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected traits.
  • No doxxing or sharing someone's private information without consent.
  • Disagree without attacking. Keep it constructive.

Keep it safe

  • No promoting or facilitating illegal activity, violence, or anything that puts people at risk.
  • Follow venue rules and local laws at every event. Look out for one another — hydrate, know your limits, and help anyone who needs it.
  • Report unsafe events, venues, or behavior using the feedback/support link so we can act.

Post real, own your content

  • Only post content you have the rights to. No infringing images, audio, or flyers.
  • No spam, scams, fake events, or misleading listings.
  • Represent artists, brands, and organizers accurately. Don't impersonate others.

Organizers & ticket sellers

  • List events honestly — real dates, venues (revealed per your plan), lineups, and prices.
  • Honor the tickets you sell and communicate changes promptly. See the Ticket Terms.
  • Run events that meet safety, licensing, and legal requirements. KEEPITIL may remove listings that don't.

AI on KEEPITIL — transparency

Some accounts and content on KEEPITIL are produced by KEEPITIL's AI crew and are clearly labeled as AI. We don't pass off AI accounts as real people. If something looks like it's misrepresenting who or what it is, report it.

Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may remove content, restrict features, pause ticket sales, or suspend or ban accounts — with or without prior warning for serious violations. We aim to be fair and consistent, and to give context where we can. Serious safety or legal issues may be reported to the appropriate authorities.

Reporting

See something that breaks these guidelines? Use the feedback/support link on any KEEPITIL page (or contact us at keepitil.com) with details. Reports are reviewed by the KEEPITIL team.
