For developers

Email us to submit your app

KEEPITIL Apps lists apps and games that have their own page on keepitil.com. To submit yours, send us one email per app or game. The KEEPITIL team reviews every submission and replies by email.

Email info@keepitil.com

The button opens your own email app. This page does not send anything itself.

What to include in your email

  • App or game name, and a one-line subtitle
  • What it does, in a few sentences
  • Where it runs today: a web link, a phone app, or both
  • A square icon (512×512 PNG) and one or two screenshots attached
  • Your privacy policy link and a support contact
  • Who it is for, and whether it is made for children
