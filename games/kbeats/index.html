How it works

FAQ

Is KBeats free?

Yes. The core catalog, competitive play and leaderboards are free. Ads, if any, never play during a song, and nothing you can buy improves your score.

Does KBeats use SoundCloud audio?

No. Gameplay audio comes only from masters artists upload directly to KBeats. SoundCloud is used only for links to artists' pages. SoundCloud does not endorse or sponsor KBeats.

My notes feel early or late.

Open Settings → Calibrate and tap along to the click. KBeats stores a separate offset for speakers, wired headphones and Bluetooth.

Who can submit music?

Anyone who controls the master, the composition (or a licence covering this use), and every sample in the track. You confirm each of these when you submit.

Are scores ranked?

Yes. Global and weekly leaderboards only list scores the KBeats server has verified by replaying your input. You can play as a guest and keep your progress when you create an account.
