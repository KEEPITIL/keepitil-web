# KEEPITIL Games and Tournaments: offer outline (one page, draft)

**Status: draft for Joe.** Prices and numbers are proposals. Nothing is listed, wired or promised. Follows the safe default in `docs/DECISIONS-2026-11-05.md`: **free entry, sponsor-funded non-cash prizes**. Not legal advice; have the official rules checked before the first night.

## The offer
KEEPITIL runs community game nights and brackets on keepitil.com, built around our own games (KMOB, KWARS, PokaSnap) and, where licensing allows, guest titles. Players enter free. **Local brands sponsor the night** and get their name in front of an engaged, local, creator-leaning audience.

## Formats

| Format | Players | Cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Game Night** | 8 to 16 | Monthly, 90 minutes | Casual rounds, hosted, short finals. Best first event. |
| **Monthly Bracket** | up to 32 | Monthly | Single-elimination, streamed finals, winners wall on the site. |
| **Charity Night** | 16 to 32 | Occasional | Free entry. Viewers are pointed to the charity's own donation page; **KEEPITIL does not collect donations**. Needs a named registered charity. |

## Sponsor tiers (proposed, per night)

| Tier | Price | You get |
|---|---|---|
| **Presenting** | $500 | "Presented by" name and logo on the page, stream and winners wall; host mention at start and finals; one sponsor-supplied prize; a short post-event report (views, entrants). 1 slot. |
| **Bracket** | $250 | Logo on the bracket page and a host mention; one prize. Up to 3 slots. |
| **Prize** | $100, or supply the prize | Named on the prize listing and winners wall. Unlimited. |
| **Community** | $50 | Name listed on the page. Good for small local shops. |
| **Series** | 3 nights for the price of 2.5 | Any tier above, booked for three nights in a row (proposed 17% off). |

Prizes are **merchandise, gift items or credit in kind**, never cash.

## What KEEPITIL provides
- The page, sign-up, bracket and a host on the night.
- The games, set up for the night (our own titles first).
- Promotion on keepitil.com, the app and our channels.
- Winners wall and a short sponsor report after each night.

## Age and rules (proposed)
- **13 and over.** Under 18 needs a parent or guardian's OK on sign-up.
- **No purchase necessary:** entry is free, and no sponsor purchase affects a player's chances.
- Skill-based play only; no chance-based prizes or drawings.
- Written **official rules** published before sign-up opens: eligibility, how winners are decided, prize list and values, and a no-cheating and conduct clause linking to the Community Guidelines.
- Prize winners are named only with their consent.
