ASHLEY CORDELIA — Of Many Mediums, One Artist

Composer, producer, recording artist and DJ — and, in parallel, cinema actress, screenwriter and director. One booking can be a DJ set, a singer, or both.

KEEPITIL

Most DJs who apply to KEEPITIL Connect list one job. Ashley Cordelia’s Instagram bio opens with a different claim entirely: “Of Many Mediums.” Underneath it, the roles stack up in her own words — “Composer • Record Producer • Recording Artist + DJ,” then “Cinema Actress, Screenwriter + Director.” That is not a branding exercise; it’s a table of contents. The Huntington Beach applicant behind the name has a dance catalogue on the streaming platforms, headline-tagged live DJ sets, a filmography, and a website that introduces her — under her legal name, Ashley Cornelia Schmitt — as “an artist, actress, model, singer, songwriter, musician, record producer, film director and DJ” with “over 15 years experience.”

The catalogue: songs first

Start with the music, because she does. Her best-known dance record is “Or Nah” — her own post for it reads “‘Or Nah’ by ASHLEY CORDELIA — Trending hard on Spotify NOW & available on all major platforms,” tagging Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL, Deezer, Amazon Music and YouTube Music. Viberate’s artist page, which tracks her dance catalogue, calls “Or Nah” one of her most recognised pieces and lists its radio edit, instrumental and acapella versions alongside the original.

The catalogue around it is wide. Viberate also names “Walk on Me,” “Runnin’” and “High On You — Original Mix,” and an extended mix of “Burn With You” — a track her own feed was still pushing in 2026: “Burn With You (Dreamstate Instrumental Version) by Ashley Cordelia OUT NOW on all major streaming and music purchase platforms.” Her live reel from Circle OC is hashtagged with the same song — #burn #burnwithyou — tying the record to the room she played it in.

Then there is the seasonal one: “Ornamental Girl,” which her bio links straight to YouTube and her feed announced with “‘Tis the Season! ‘Ornamental Girl’ OUT NOW (link in bio).” And the newer singles keep coming: “Wait 4 Me,” released January 1, 2025 on S&M Records per Shazam — which also credits the songwriting to Ashley Cornelia Schmitt — and linked from her bio via YouTube Music; “Secret Garden”; “SHE MOVES ME”; and “THE RULE,” which her own post credits plainly: “Produced and Written by @ashley.cordelia & King Karlton. Sound Engineering by @nu3tron for @pinnaclesoundoc.”

The collaborations fill out the edges: “Higher Vibrations (feat. C4MULA),” from which she quoted her own verse — “Physical beauty’s the spark, the igniter… but means nothing when you go know someone higher…” — the EDM duet “no one else like you” with Carson Allen (“OUT NOW on all streaming platforms… #edmduet #producer #dj”), “make me feel,” out on iTunes, Amazon Music and all major platforms, and “Take 2 To Tango” with DJ Zole. Shazam’s credits pages add two more titles to the solo catalogue, “Kiss Me Now” and “I Can’t Help Myself (danse mix),” both with songwriting credited to Ashley Cornelia Schmitt. For a booker, the point is simple: this is an artist who can walk into a DJ booking carrying a catalogue of her own records — and, as below, sometimes plays nothing else.

The live DJ: all-original, headline-tagged

Her live receipts are on her own feed. A reel captioned “Live @circleoc” carries the tags that tell the story — #headliner #original #originalartist #femaleproducer #nowbooking — closing with “one for the books.” For a Projekt Raves / I Feel Diskonnected desert date she announced: “I play Saturday @projektraves @ifeeldiskonnected… thank you to @_dsknkt_ for opportunity! I will be playing an all-original set for you!” An all-original set is a flex most DJs can’t make; it requires the catalogue above.

In Los Angeles she is part of the Helipad Society run of outdoor parties — “HELIPAD SOCIETY! I can’t wait to dance with you!!! August 31. Elysian Park.” — and posted from its 30th edition at Angel’s Point, Elysian Park, a run of Sunday sessions she promoted through the week. And in July 2026 she was on her own feed promoting KEEPITIL’s beach day: “12 July 2026. @keepitil — KEEP IT SUMMER — See you babes there.” Like so many artists in this queue, she found KEEPITIL by playing in its orbit first.

Her application lists her DJ genres as House, Tech House and Speed Garage. Her released catalogue leans broader — dance, pop and EDM singles with her own vocals and writing on them — which is precisely the combination her January 2026 booking post sells: “Now accepting TV/film/theatre roles, musical collaboration requests, concert bookings for DJ or singer, voice over acting gigs, and any creative collaboration inquiries.”

The screen life

The second career is documented just as publicly. IMDbPro’s profile for her lists her as “Actress, Producer, Writer” and names her known-for titles: The Sacrifice (2016), Road to Romance (2023) and Raising a Vampire (2020). Back in December 2013 she was already sitting for interviews as a working multi-hyphenate — ActorsE Chat host Kurt Kelly’s show billed her as a performer, singer, actress, designer and model who “has also worked as a producer, director, actress, and art designer.”

The screen work is current, not archival. Her feed carries her lead role in “The Fabulous Lady T,” a Lime Street and Friends entry for the 48 Hour Film Project, Los Angeles — she played the lead, in a collaboration her posts credit to Lime Street Entertainment, Goldthunder Productions, Schmittfilms and Jessica May Jensen Studios, screening at the Regal NoHo. Her own production name is right there in the credits: Schmittfilms. And her bio’s shortest line may be the most personal credit of all: “Psalm 23.”

The artist behind the name

Her own website handles the formal introduction: “Ashley Cornelia Schmitt, known professionally as Ashley Cordelia, is a Los Angeles based artist, actress, model, singer, songwriter, musician, record producer, film director and DJ. She has over 15 years experience and has shone a light on many subjects in society and our world.” Her application puts her home base in Huntington Beach; between the two, the honest summary is a Southern California artist whose work runs from OC rooms to LA film sets and Elysian Park party points — helipads, in this case.

Her fans have a name, and she uses it herself: the hashtags on her posts rally the #ashtronauts. And she is openly building the next phase in public, down to a direct invitation to other creators: “i would love it if you used my music in your posts and reels here on ig, tiktok, youtube, facebook, etc.”

  • Headline experience: live at Circle OC, tagged #headliner in her own recap; Helipad Society sessions at Elysian Park, Los Angeles

The quick version

ASHLEY CORDELIA (Ashley Cornelia Schmitt) is a Huntington Beach–submitted, Southern California composer, record producer, recording artist and DJ — and, in parallel, a cinema actress, screenwriter and director with over 15 years’ experience per her own site. Her dance catalogue (“Or Nah,” “Burn With You,” “Ornamental Girl,” “Wait 4 Me”) plays on all major platforms; live, she has headlined at Circle OC, played an all-original set for Projekt Raves, and joined the Helipad Society sessions in Elysian Park. One booking can be a DJ set, a singer, or both.
