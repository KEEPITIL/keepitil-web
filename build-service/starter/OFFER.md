## What every site includes

- Mobile-first, readable at phone width, with dark and light modes.
- No trackers, no ads, no third-party scripts, no web fonts.
- Title, description, canonical URL and social preview set for every page.
- Accessible by default: skip link, visible focus, 44 px tap targets, reduced-motion respected.
- Your site files are yours. You get the built pages and the `site.json` that makes them.

## Delivery steps

1. **Brief (day 0).** You fill in the intake list below. We confirm the tier and the date in writing.
2. **Payment.** 50% to start, 50% before launch. (Payment method is a placeholder until Joe sets it up; no payments are wired.)
3. **First draft (day 3 for Starter, day 6 Standard, day 9 Pro).** A private preview link you can open on your phone.
4. **Revisions.** You send all feedback in one message per round. We apply it within 2 business days.
5. **Launch check.** Lighthouse on mobile (95+ on all four scores), form test, links test.
6. **Go live.** We publish it or hand you the files, then confirm the live URL.

## What you provide

- Business name, one-line tagline, and a short description.
- Contact email (and phone or address if you want them shown).
- Your logo (PNG or SVG) and two brand colours, or tell us and we pick.
- Text for each page, or notes we can turn into text (Pro).
- Photos you own or have the right to use. We don't supply stock photos.
- Your domain name and login, or permission for us to help you register one (the domain itself is paid by you).
- One person who can approve changes.

## Revisions policy

- A **revision round** is one batch of changes sent together. Each tier includes the rounds listed above.
- Wording, colour, image and layout tweaks inside the agreed pages count as revisions.
- **Not a revision:** new pages, a new design direction after sign-off, new features such as shops or logins, or content that arrives after launch. These are quoted separately (proposed hourly rate: **$60**), always in writing before work starts.
- Extra rounds beyond the included ones: **$90 per round**.

## Not included

Online shops or payments, member logins, custom databases, multi-language sites, SEO retainers and ad management. Ask and we will quote them as a separate project.
