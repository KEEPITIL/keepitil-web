Traffic Pop Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026 · Applies to the Traffic Pop app for iPhone and the web version at keepitil.com/games/trafficpop/

Data Not Collected

Traffic Pop does not collect any data from you. KEEPITIL does not receive, store, sell or share anything about you or how you play.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following only on your own device:

  • Unlocked levels, stars and helpers
  • The level you are playing, so you can continue it
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, animation speed, reduced motion)
  • Playtest stats (attempts, wins, times and helper use per level), shown to you in Settings

None of this is ever sent anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing this site's data in your browser) erases it.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads and no in-app purchases
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers
  • No contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos
  • The iPhone app makes no internet connections at all; everything it needs is built in

The web version

The web version uses the same on-device storage and sends nothing back while you play. Like any website, keepitil.com is delivered by a hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which may keep standard technical server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. KEEPITIL does not receive or use those logs for Traffic Pop.

Children

Traffic Pop is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online features are added, we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before the change ships.
