Organizer fees & payouts

KEEPITIL · Last updated November 27, 2026

This page is for people who sell tickets on KEEPITIL. It shows what buyers pay on top of your ticket price, how that splits between Stripe and KEEPITIL, how you get paid, what happens when a ticket is refunded or disputed, and what is not allowed. Numbers come from how KEEPITIL ticketing works and from Stripe's own pages, which are linked.

1. The fee: the buyer pays it, you receive the full ticket price

At checkout the buyer pays your ticket price plus a service fee of 5% + $0.50 per order. The fee is shown to the buyer before they pay. You receive the full ticket price you set: no fee is taken out of your ticket money. Refunds, disputes and Stripe's Connect charges are the exception: they are deducted from your payouts (section 3). Free and RSVP tickets are never charged and carry no fee.

The service fee has two lines, so you can see where it goes:

LineStripe processing feeKEEPITIL operational feeTotal service fee

  • The $0.50 is charged once per order, not per ticket. The percentage is on the paid tickets in the order.
  • Currency conversion: the extra 2% is added when the tickets are priced in a currency other than US dollars. All tickets on KEEPITIL are priced in US dollars today.
  • International cards: Stripe charges +1.5% on them. KEEPITIL cannot see a card's country before the buyer pays, so the buyer pays the same 5% + $0.50 on every card and the extra international fee is not added.
  • The “KEEPITIL operational fee” is KEEPITIL's share: it covers running ticketing, payouts and support.

2. Stripe's fees, payouts and getting set up

  • Card processing: Stripe's published price is 2.9% + 30¢ per successful domestic card payment, plus 1.5% for international cards and plus 1% if currency conversion is needed (stripe.com/pricing). On KEEPITIL the buyer's service fee covers it: you pay no processing fee on ticket sales (Stripe: destination charges: the platform pays the Stripe fee on the charge).
  • Your Stripe account: when you set up payouts, KEEPITIL creates a Stripe Express account for you and sends you to Stripe to finish. You give Stripe your identity and bank details there, not to KEEPITIL. Stripe asks for information about you and, for a business, the business: for example name, address, tax ID number, date of birth, and details of beneficial owners. It may also ask for a scan of a government ID or proof of address, and it “temporarily pauses charges or payouts if the information isn't provided or verified” (Stripe: identity verification).
  • You cannot sell paid tickets until payouts are set up. Checkout refuses a paid ticket for an organizer who has not finished Stripe's setup.
  • When you are paid: by default, money from a sale builds up in your Stripe balance and is paid out to your bank on a daily rolling basis (Stripe: payouts for connected accounts). Stripe typically schedules a new account's first payout within 7 to 14 days (Stripe: payouts). Payout settings and status are managed with Stripe.

3. Refunds, disputes and chargebacks: the organizer pays

The organizer pays for refunds, disputes and Stripe's related charges, and KEEPITIL deducts them from the organizer's payout. That means:

  • Refunds: the amount refunded to a buyer comes out of your payout. See the Refund Policy: a refund returns the buyer's ticket price to their original payment method.
  • Stripe's lost processing fee: Stripe does not give back its own fee on a refund (Stripe: refunds), so that fee is also deducted from your payout.
  • Disputes (chargebacks): if a buyer disputes a payment with their bank, the payment is reversed right away (Stripe: disputes). If the dispute is lost, the disputed amount is deducted from your payout, plus Stripe's dispute fees: $15 when a dispute is received and $15 if it is countered (the counter fee comes back if the dispute is won), as shown on stripe.com/pricing. The ticket may be voided (Ticket Terms).
  • Stripe Connect charges: any charges Stripe makes for your account or your payouts are deducted from your payouts too. Stripe's published Connect pricing is $2 per monthly active account and 0.25% + 25¢ per payout (stripe.com/connect/pricing).

The same rule is in the Ticket Terms (organizer responsibilities) and the Terms of Service.

4. Stripe's restrictions: what cannot be sold

All card payments on KEEPITIL run on Stripe, so Stripe's list of prohibited and restricted businesses applies to every paid ticket and every organizer. Stripe can change this list, and can modify or revoke approval at any time. Stripe says its list gives examples and is not complete. The live list is the source of truth: stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses. This is our plain-words summary of the parts that can touch an event or a ticket (read from Stripe's list on November 27, 2026; Stripe's page says it was last updated 2026-09-22).

Prohibited: Stripe does not allow these at all

  • Cannabis (marijuana): cannabis products, dispensaries and related businesses, CBD above the legal THC limit, equipment for growing cannabis, and courses on growing it. KEEPITIL does not host or sell tickets to cannabis events. Cannabis events and businesses belong on Find My Weed (findmyweedapp.com).
  • Illegal products and services: illegal drugs and look-alike substances, drug paraphernalia, fake IDs, signal-jamming gear, promoting unlawful violence, or anything illegal where you operate or sell.
  • Adult content and services: sexual services, strip clubs and topless bars, adult chat and fetish services, pornography and other content made for sexual gratification.
  • Gambling and prize games: games of chance, sweepstakes and contests with a money or goods prize; games of skill with a prize, such as game tournaments, darts, card or board games; entry or player fees that promise a prize; betting and odds-making; lotteries; bidding-fee auctions. Do not sell tickets to raffles, lotteries, prize draws or prize tournaments.
  • Weapons and dangerous materials: illegal weapons, replica or toy firearms that are not marked as required, toxic, flammable or radioactive materials, and goods the postal service will not carry.
  • Counterfeit and unlicensed goods: bootleg or unauthorized music, movies and software, counterfeit or fake brand-name goods, and anything that infringes someone's trademark, copyright or patent.
  • Financial schemes and risky finance: pyramid schemes, multi-level marketing that pays for recruiting, get-rich-quick schemes, deceptive or high-pressure selling, resale that adds no value, predatory investment pitches, debt relief, credit repair, check cashing, person-to-person money transfer, and cryptocurrency mining, token sales (ICOs) or secondary NFT sales.
  • Other prohibited categories: government services without authorization, identity-theft protection, bail bonds and some legal services, commercial airlines, cruises and timeshares, and unsafe supplements or fake medicines.

Restricted: Stripe allows these only after extra review

  • Fundraising: charities, political groups and anyone offering a reward for donations; crowdfunding; charity raffles and sweepstakes (only in some countries).
  • Tobacco and CBD: tobacco, e-cigarettes and e-liquid sold within the law, and CBD with only negligible THC.
  • Legal firearms and other weapons: guns, regulated parts, stun guns, swords, pepper spray and similar.
  • Health: online pharmacies, prescription products, regulated medical devices and telemedicine.
  • Money and stored value: investment, lending, money transfer, escrow, buy-now-pay-later, cryptocurrency, gift cards, game currency, precious metals and other high-value goods, and platforms that process payments for others.
  • Other restricted categories: platforms that let creators sell exclusive content, dating services, file-sharing services, and travel reservation services.

Stripe's list also bars use of its services for certain countries and regions that are under sanctions. Alcohol is not on Stripe's US list, but alcohol, age and venue licensing laws still apply to your event.

If Stripe decides an event or organizer is not allowed, it can refuse or stop payments, hold money or close the account, and KEEPITIL cannot reverse that. Questions: info@keepitil.com.

5. KEEPITIL's rules

  • KEEPITIL does not host or sell tickets to cannabis events. Cannabis events and businesses belong on Find My Weed (findmyweedapp.com).
  • You must be at least 18, or the age of majority where you live, and have a KEEPITIL account (Terms). Buyers also need a free KEEPITIL account to buy a ticket.
  • You must finish Stripe's identity and bank setup before you can sell paid tickets, and have the right to sell tickets to the event (Ticket Terms, section 6).
  • Paid tickets must cost at least $1.00. Prices are in US dollars. A buyer can add up to 20 tickets of one type to an order.
  • An event must be published (new events are reviewed first), and tickets cannot be bought once the event has started or outside the sales dates you set. Sold-out tiers stop selling.
  • An event that has sold tickets cannot be deleted. Refund every ticket first; then the event can be hidden.
  • You must describe the event accurately, honor valid tickets and follow venue, licensing, safety and local laws, including age limits such as 18+ or 21+ (Ticket Terms). The Community Guidelines prohibit promoting illegal activity.
  • KEEPITIL can pause ticket sales on the whole site.
