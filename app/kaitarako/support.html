Kai Tarako help and support

Kai Tarako is a free letter-tracing app for young children, made by KEEPITIL LLC. It has no ads and no in-app purchases. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and nothing your child does in the app is sent to us or to anyone else. Progress stays on the device.

Contact us

Email info@keepitil.com. A grown-up should write to us; please don’t include your child’s name or other details about them.

Common questions

How do I open the Grown-ups area? Press and hold the Grown-ups button for 3 seconds, then answer the times-table question. With VoiceOver, Switch Control or a keyboard, activating the button goes straight to the question.

How do I move progress to a new device? Use Grown-ups → Save backup on the old device and save the file wherever you choose, using your device’s own save or share options. Then use Restore backup on the new device. We never receive the file.

How do I delete everything? Grown-ups → Privacy → Delete all progress removes all progress and settings from the device, and deleting the app removes everything it saved. Any backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them. We hold no information about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
