STRATEGY YOU CAN FEEL

More than a tower defense game. This is your battlefield.

KMOB combines the rush of an endless survival game with deliberate troop choices. Your army isn't a decoration: how you build it, protect it, and spend your resources decides whether you make it through the next wave.

▦

Plan your army

Build a preset across six soldier classes with a 300-point weighted capacity. Change your War Plan during battle as the enemy changes tactics.

◎

Command the tank

Slide to line up your cannon, switch between automatic and manual deployment, and decide when your army attacks or holds the line.

✧

Earn your next advantage

Spend battle gold on soldiers and upgrades. Defeat bosses to earn rewards and choose lasting bonuses that shape future fights.

⚡

Four elemental powers

Fire burns, Ice slows, Poison weakens healing, and Lightning jumps between enemies. Equipped skins give your strategy a new layer.

✺

See the bigger battle

Zoom out up to 3× for a wider view or accelerate play up to 5× when you're ready to move faster. Your choices still matter.

∞

No final wave

The fight gets tougher as your army grows. Aim for a new personal best, experiment with different plans, and come back smarter.

IPHONE · SAFARI

Add KMOB to your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮).

  4. Confirm, then launch KMOB from your home screen.

The button name varies by Android and browser version. Home-screen installation is not a native store download.

PRIVACY

KMOB privacy statement

Last updated: 18 October 2026. KMOB: Endless War is published by KEEPITIL. KMOB has no ads and no tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share player data. You can play without an account. An optional account is used only for purchased Diamonds and War Tokens.

KMOB for iPhone (app)

Free play runs entirely on your iPhone and works offline without an account. Your progress, settings and saved battle are stored on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Optional account (Sign in with Apple)

Signing in is needed only to buy Diamonds or War Tokens, so purchases are kept safe and follow you to a new phone. On iPhone you sign in with Apple. When you sign in we store your email address (or Apple's private relay address), a user ID, and your purchased Diamond and War Token balance with its history. Your sign-in is a KEEPITIL account hosted by our database provider, Supabase, in the United States.

In-app purchases

On iPhone, Diamonds and War Tokens are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we receive Apple's signed record of the purchase (product, transaction ID, date, price and whether it was a test or live purchase). We use purchase records only to credit your balance, handle refunds and prevent fraud. Apple decides refunds; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a purchase is refunded, the matching currency is removed from your balance.

Deleting your account

In the app, open SHOP, then BUY DIAMONDS & WAR TOKENS, and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your KMOB player data and purchased balance and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access. Purchase records are kept with your identity removed where the law requires it. Your KEEPITIL sign-in can be shared with other KEEPITIL apps; to delete it as well, email info@keepitil.com.

KMOB in your web browser (this page)

Your progress and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. To help us balance and fix the game, the browser version sends one anonymous record per battle to our database host (Supabase): a random run code, the game version, a coarse device type (iPhone, Android or desktop), processor core count, memory class, screen size, graphics and speed settings, gameplay numbers (waves, time, soldiers, upgrades) and performance timings. Weekly and all-time leaderboards store a random 8-character player code with your wave, time and bosses defeated. None of this includes your name, email, contacts or location. Like any website, the servers that deliver the page, its fonts (Google Fonts) and this data see your device's IP address as part of the connection.

Your choices

Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your web progress and player code. Deleting the iPhone app deletes its saved progress. For questions about privacy, or to ask us to remove leaderboard entries, email info@keepitil.com.
