KEEPITIL PRESENTS

Steezy Ceezy — House, Disco and a Home Crew in Orange County

House, disco and open format from La Mirada — a Thursday resident in two counties who learned the craft in public, one first at a time.

KEEPITIL

Some DJs arrive with a press kit. Steezy Ceezy arrived with a paper trail — five years of it, posted in public, one first at a time. First time on CDJs, January 2022: “First time on cdjs! So much fun but a lot to learn!” First festival, that October in Lancaster. First crew he could call home, Stack’d House LA, thanked by name in his New Year post closing out 2024: “@stackdhousela for giving me a place to call home.” Follow the posts in order and you watch a La Mirada house DJ build himself the slow, verifiable way — Thursday residencies in Koreatown, a secret rave room inside the Yost Theater in Santa Ana, pre-parties for Skyline and HARD Summer, and a sound his own bio states without hedging: “Disco/Nudisco, house and Open Format DJ.”

Steezy Ceezy is Ceasar Gardea, of La Mirada. His KEEPITIL application lists his genres in the order a booker should hear them — house, tech house, nu disco/disco — and his Instagram, 131 posts deep, backs every word of it with a dated receipt.

Learning in public

The earliest layer of the feed is a DJ teaching himself out loud. Through late 2020 and 2021 his page is IGTV mixes — Christmas house remixes (“Couple of your classic Christmas songs with a twist”), Sunday starters, short sets posted as he made them. There is no gap in the record where a polished act suddenly appears; the January 2022 CDJ post is the hinge, a DJ announcing new gear and a learning curve in the same sentence. Ten months later came the milestone he flagged himself: opening the @ladypinkpanther_ stage at Social Desert Camp in October 2022 — “had a blast doing my first festival” — with ticket links sold through Posh for the October 15 Social Desert Festival date in Lancaster under his own name.

The personal posts around the work explain the stamina. He has been going to U2 shows with his dad “since I was about 10/11 years old” — a Sphere show in October 2023 was “one of the best @u2 shows I’ve been to,” shared with his father. EDC Las Vegas is his annual reset: in May 2026 he wrote that “EDC always will have a special place in my heart! The one time a year when I get to just feel like a kid again.” A DJ whose fandom is that documented tends to program like a fan — which is another way of saying, for the floor.

A home: Stack’d House LA

Every working DJ needs a crew that answers the phone, and Steezy Ceezy’s is in his bio: @stackdhousela. Stack’d House LA describes itself simply — “Join The Team / DJ / Send Us A DM For Info” — and his posts show what membership means in practice. In November 2024 the crew took over The Speakeasy OC: his own announcement puts him on at 12 a.m.–1 a.m. on Friday, November 15 “with none other than @stackdhousela over at @thespeakeasyoc,” tickets at $5, with a second show the next night, November 16, in Pomona. The crew’s own save-the-date for that Speakeasy night lists the family on one flyer — Oso Aiff, Steezy Ceezy, DJ Nestog, Hot Pursuit, SlayyThaBeat, Mister Apizzle — ladies free all night with RSVP, music from 11 p.m. His January 1, 2025 reflection closes the loop: alongside thanks to Electric Jungle and Novacane Sounds “for letting me play at one of their events and one of my favorite events played to date,” the Stack’d House line stands out — a place to call home, and “the vision is there for 2025 to make it our year.”

Thursdays: K-Town Boogie and the Electric Jungle

If the crew is the home, Thursdays are the job. In Koreatown, Los Angeles, the K-Town Boogie Thursdays put him in a resident’s chair: the collective’s January 2024 recap thanks supporters and announces that resident DJ @from85_romeo would celebrate his birthday doing a b2b with @steezyceezy1, soju towers and all. His own February 2024 reel sells the room’s range in one line — DJs “Spinning house, disco, Latin house, bass house and everything in between,” free entry, every Thursday from 8 p.m. That “everything in between” is the open-format half of his bio, documented as a weekly reality rather than a claim.

In Orange County, the Thursday institution is Electric Jungle at the Yost Theater in downtown Santa Ana — a weekly series documented as running Thursdays 10 p.m.–2 a.m. upstairs in the Yost’s Paradise Room, house music all night. Electric Jungle’s own November 2025 flyer for “Techno & Tequila” night (Thursday, November 6, Yost Theater, 307 N Spurgeon St, Santa Ana) bills the arc of the evening — “We’re starting the night with house and ending with techno a full journey through the Electric Jungle” — and lists the lineup in order: PIXEL · STEEZY CEEZY · UNANGEL · DIZZIE, 18+, no cover before 11 p.m. By February 2026 he was posting the Yost’s Secret Rave Room himself — “The Yost has a Secret Rave Room I wanna tell you about” — a resident’s kind of secret to share.

The circuit around the residencies

Around those anchors, the one-off bookings map the wider Southern California working circuit: a Cinco de Mayo weekend party at Pattern Bar, DTLA (May 2025, from 4 p.m.); a Valentine’s night in Santa Ana (February 2025) — “Come fall in LOVE with your favorite dj!” with his own sweetener, “I’m buying the first round of shots for anybody that comes out to support”; a HARD Summer pre-party at Cosmo Ultra Lounge, Pomona (July 2024), plus the July 3 Electric Jungle rave at the same room; a two-stage birthday bill at The W, Riverside (November 2025) built to celebrate his and Father House’s birthdays, house on one stage and open format on the other; and the Unofficial Skyline Pre-Party at The S OC (February 27, 2026), where his own posts put his house set at 1–2 a.m. — “Tonight we dance babyyyyy!!” Earlier form agrees: a Jupiter Thursdays back-to-back at 3:10–4:10 a.m. in December 2022, and a November 24, 2023 show with his set at 1–2 a.m. The late slot is not new for him; it is the through-line.

The sound

His application and his bio agree, which is rarer than it should be: house first, tech house beside it, nu disco and disco underneath, open format when the room asks. The K-Town Boogie reels show the blend as programming — house, disco and Latin house in one Thursday — and the Electric Jungle flyer shows the other direction, a house-to-techno journey across a single night. What the record does not contain is a streaming catalogue this research could verify: no SoundCloud, Mixcloud or Spotify artist link is published from his profiles beyond his Linktree. The sound, for now, lives where he plays it — in rooms, on Thursdays, at 1 a.m.

Why book Steezy Ceezy

  • Residency-proven: a K-Town Boogie Thursday resident in Los Angeles (documented b2b with resident DJ Romeo, Jan 2024) and an Electric Jungle name at the Yost Theater, Santa Ana — including its Secret Rave Room / Paradise Room Thursdays
  • A crew behind the booking: Stack’d House LA is in his bio and on his flyers — The Speakeasy OC takeover (Nov 15, 2024, his set 12–1 a.m.) is a crew night, not a solo gamble
  • The late slot is his natural habitat: 1–2 a.m. at the Skyline pre-party (The S OC, Feb 2026), 12–1 a.m. at The Speakeasy, 3:10–4:10 a.m. b2b at Jupiter Thursdays
  • Range a booker can actually use: house / tech house / nu disco / disco at the core, with documented open-format and Latin house Thursdays, and a house-to-techno journey set (Electric Jungle, Nov 2025)
  • He promotes the room, in his own voice: pre-party announcements, ticket links in bio, and a Valentine’s post offering his supporters the first round
  • A five-year public paper trail: first CDJs (Jan 2022) to first festival (Social Desert, Oct 2022) to two-county Thursdays — every step dated on his own page

The quick version

Steezy Ceezy (Ceasar Gardea) is a La Mirada house, tech house and nu disco/disco DJ — open format when the room calls for it — who built his career in public: IGTV mixes in 2020–21, first CDJs in January 2022, a first festival that October, a home crew in Stack’d House LA, a Thursday residency at K-Town Boogie in Los Angeles and Electric Jungle nights at the Yost Theater in Santa Ana, capped by a 1–2 a.m. house set at the unofficial Skyline pre-party in February 2026. He is, in the best sense, a working DJ: the name on the Thursday flyer, in two counties at once.
