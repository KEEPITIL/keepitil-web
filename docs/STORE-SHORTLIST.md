# KEEPITIL store proposal: improvements shortlist

A short list of store improvements, ranked by expected lift against effort. These are proposals only. Nothing here changes prices or offers, and none of it is live.

## Ranked shortlist

| # | Improvement | Lift | Effort | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Look at the funnel first:** review Shopify Analytics (sessions to /shop, product views, add-to-cart, checkout starts) for the last 90 days | Enables the rest | 15 minutes, no change | Shows whether the issue is visitors or conversion before anything else is changed. |
| 2 | **Lead /shop with the digital products** (Release Strategy Session, Graphics Pack, Rollout Kit, SoundCloud Audit, Lyric Pack) above merch | Medium to high | Low (page order and a banner) | Put the offers that suit artists where visitors land. |
| 3 | **Turn the free Rollout Kit into a lead magnet:** ask for email consent and send a short follow-up pointing to the paid products | Medium | Medium | Needs consent wording and a sending tool. |
| 4 | **Collections cleanup:** merge the two tee collections, retire catch-all collections, and lead with the short "KEEPITIL" collection | Medium | Low | A short, clear first collection beats a wall of hundreds of items. |
| 5 | **Flagship product-page copy** for the KEEPITIL items: fit, fabric, sizing, delivery estimate, returns, one real photo | Medium | Low to medium | Much of the catalogue still uses generic print-on-demand description text. |
| 6 | **Shipping messaging:** state the real shipping cost and delivery window on product pages and the cart | Low to medium | Low | Cheap clarity at the decision point. The shipping amount quoted on the site should match what checkout charges. |
