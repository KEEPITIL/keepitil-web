Refund Policy

KEEPITIL · Last updated November 27, 2026

Event tickets

What you pay

You need a free KEEPITIL account to buy a ticket. You pay the ticket price shown, by card through Stripe Checkout, and nothing is added at checkout. The organizer is paid through Stripe, and KEEPITIL keeps a service fee out of the organizer's share (2.5% plus $0.49 per ticket under $10, or $0.99 per ticket at $10 or more). Because that fee is not charged to you, a refund returns the full ticket price you paid.

How to ask for a refund

Send the order details to keepitil.com/support (choose Billing) or info@keepitil.com: the email on your KEEPITIL account, the event name and the date you bought. There is no refund button in your account. KEEPITIL issues the refund in Stripe. A refund can only go back to the card or payment method you paid with; Stripe cannot send it to a different card or bank account.

  • For KEEPITIL-hosted events, tickets are refundable up to 48 hours before the event start, unless the ticket is marked non-refundable at purchase.
  • Third-party events follow the organizer's own refund rules.

When a refund is issued

As soon as Stripe records the refund, your ticket is marked refunded on KEEPITIL, it stops being valid, and the seat goes back on sale. Stripe says you will see the refund as a credit about 5 to 10 business days later, depending on your bank.

A refund can be for the full amount or for part of it. Unless we tell you otherwise, a ticket refund is for the full ticket price you paid. Stripe does not give its own card-processing fee back to us when a payment is refunded, and we do not take that fee out of your refund.

If an event is canceled or postponed

  • Canceled: you are entitled to a refund of the ticket price. An organizer cannot delete an event that has sold tickets: they refund every ticket first, and only then can the event be hidden.
  • Postponed or rescheduled: your ticket is typically valid for the new date. If you cannot attend the new date, ask for a refund as above, and it follows this policy and the organizer's stated rules.

If you ask your bank to dispute the charge instead of asking us (a chargeback), the payment is reversed right away and the ticket may be voided (see the Ticket Terms). Please contact us first. Free RSVP tickets are never charged, so there is nothing to refund.

Challenge / contest entries

Entry fees are refundable before the challenge start; once a challenge has begun, entries are non-refundable except where required by law.

Partner links, rewards & merchandise

EARN partner offers, reward programs, and the KEEPITIL apparel store are operated by other companies, and you pay those companies directly — KEEPITIL does not process those payments and cannot refund them. Refunds and cancellations for anything bought there follow that retailer's or partner's own policy. Rewards earned through a partner program are issued by that partner under its terms, not purchased from KEEPITIL, so there is nothing for KEEPITIL to refund.

Other fees

Service or listing fees are generally non-refundable once the service has been provided. If something went wrong on our end, contact support and we'll make it right.
