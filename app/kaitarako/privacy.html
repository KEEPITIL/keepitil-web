Kai Tarako privacy policy

Kai Tarako is a letter-tracing app for young children. The child never has an account. A parent or guardian can optionally create one, and only after a parent gives verified consent. This page says, in plain words, what is stored and why.

Who has an account

Only parents and guardians. Sign-in is by an emailed code; there is no password. Children never sign in, and the app never asks a child for a name, email, birth date, photo, voice recording or location.

What we store

  • Parent: the email address used to sign in.
  • Child profile: an avatar picture and a colour chosen from fixed lists, owned by the parent. No name, email, birth date or photo.
  • Progress, per child profile: lesson stars, completions and practice days.
  • Consent record: when a parent requests, verifies, or withdraws consent, and when a child profile is added, changed or deleted, we add an entry to an append-only log. The log holds ids, the event, the consent scope and the policy version. It holds no content and no personal details, and it is kept as proof of consent even after an account is deleted.

We do not save the drawings or finger paths a child makes.

Parental consent

Before anything about a child leaves the device, we ask the parent for consent. A parent reads a notice, then confirms with a second emailed code (valid for 15 minutes) and a confirmation email sent after a delay. Our server verifies the consent; a parent’s own device cannot approve it. Until consent is verified, nothing about a child is stored on our servers. Consent is given per purpose, so a parent can agree to one and not another.

Where it is kept

Account data is kept in KEEPITIL’s own database in the United States, in a separate area used only by Kai Tarako. Only the parent’s own account can read or change a child profile it owns. Kai Tarako uses no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and the child app talks only to KEEPITIL’s own service.

How the data is used

To save and sync progress under the parent’s account. With a separate parental consent, de-identified and aggregated patterns (not any one child) may be used to improve what the app teaches. There is no chat for children.

Teachers

Sharing progress with teachers is off. If it is ever offered, it will need its own separate consent from the parent before anything is shared.

Withdrawing consent and deleting data

  • In the Grown-ups area, Stop saving withdraws consent and stops syncing. No new or changed child data is stored afterwards.
  • Delete child removes that child profile and its progress.
  • Delete account removes the parent account and all child data.
  • Accounts not used for 24 months are deleted automatically.
  • Deleting the app removes what it saved on the device. A backup file you saved stays where you put it until you delete it.

On the device

The app also keeps settings and progress on the device itself so a child can pick up where they left off, and the Grown-ups area sits behind a grown-ups gate (hold the button for 3 seconds and answer a times-table question). Without an account, none of this leaves the device.

Parents’ rights

A parent decides whether a child uses Kai Tarako and whether an account exists. A parent can review, delete, or stop us collecting a child’s information at any time using the controls above, or by emailing info@keepitil.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date, and ask a parent again where the law requires it.
