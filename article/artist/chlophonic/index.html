KEEPITIL PRESENTS

Chlophonic — Pastel Colors, Filthy Drops

Sugar on the surface, bass in the booth — a kawaii universe wrapped around a genuinely heavy multi-genre sound.

KEEPITIL

The first thing you notice is the color. Pastel dresses, candy emojis, a bunny mascot energy, a self-named universe — the “Chlosphere” — that runs on kawaii. The second thing you notice is the sound, and it is not cute at all: dubstep drops she herself advertises as “filthy,” headbanger sets, a drum & bass rinse-out, a techno debut. Chlophonic has built one of the most recognizable acts in the Southern California underground out of that exact contradiction — sugar on the surface, bass in the booth — and October 2026 shows what it adds up to: five bookings in a single month, from a Hollywood Thai-community night to a Halloween headline set in DTLA to a full bass-music bill at Dave & Buster’s in Ontario.

Thai-born, SoCal-built

Chlophonic is a Thai-born DJ based in Southern California — that’s how Otaku Rave Society introduced her in its Community Spotlight 015, adding that she brings “playful energy through Dubstep, Bass House, Future Bass, and Anime inspired EDM.” Her application to KEEPITIL lists Santa Ana as the city she represents, and the Thai roots aren’t a bio line — they’re a booking pattern. In April 2026 she opened for The Illusion Night at Thai New Year LA, writing that “playing in Thai Town hit different.” In October she played the 1% night at Kruang Tedd in Hollywood alongside Millusion, a Friday built — in the event’s own bilingual billing — around music, new friends, and the Thai community coming together.

The crew that carried her there is named in her own New Year reflection: Team Discotheque, whom she thanked for “believing in me since day one” and giving her “so many opportunities to DJ.” Her manager is @ronnievibez, whom she calls both her manager and her best friend — the person behind her photoshoots, vlogs, and events. It’s an unusually well-documented support system for an underground DJ, and it explains the polish: this is an act run like a small company, with management, media, and merch all in-house.

The sound: four genres, one setting — hers

Her KEEPITIL application lists her genres plainly: bass, dubstep, house, DnB. Her Instagram bio adds the fifth ingredient that ties it together: anime. Her official Ravengelion event bio describes “a Southern California–based DJ known for blending anime and J-culture influences with high-energy electronic music,” whose “sets move through kawaii future bass, dubstep, bass house, and anime remixes, bringing a playful, colorful energy to conventions, raves, and fan-focused events.”

The range is real, and it’s set-by-set, not theoretical. She has played a dedicated drum & bass set at Bruno’s Bar and Grille — thanking the promoter for “spaces that let me experiment, push my sound, and go a little faster than usual.” She played a city pop set for B Kawaii Market at the Little Tokyo Galleria. She headlined a house-music Halloween night bill at Brite Star Tavern. And in June 2026 she played her first official techno set — at BLEU Nightclub OC, for KEEPITIL’s own Keep It Techno night (see below). Her NijiFest bio puts the philosophy in one line: sets built to balance “heavy drops with melodic storytelling,” on a mission to “make people feel alive, connected, and fully in the moment.”

The rooms

The calendar reads like a tour of every corner of the SoCal scene at once:

  • Banana Bay, Rowland Heights is her most recurring room — The Illusion Night’s EDM Thursdays keep bringing her back: a Fourth of July bass party (10:30–11:15 slot), a September Techno Thursday, and an October Moon Illusion Spooky Month set where she went full Joker, in costume, for the Halloween run.
  • High Tide DTLA bookends her year — she opened for Usagi there in April (“these are the kind of nights that remind me why I do this”), then returned in October as a headliner of the Spooky Disco Luau, atop a bill celebrating one year of the community’s zine.
  • The Compound LA is her anime-circuit home base: the Ravengelion anime rave in September 2026, and the Chiikawa Rave in July, where she ran her first-ever merch booth.
  • BLEU Nightclub OC, Westminster hosted the milestone: on June 12, 2026, KEEPITIL’s Keep It Techno night — opening alongside PYPRR for headliner KriStyle — was, in her words, her “official first techno set.” “Thank you @keepitil for the opportunity,” she posted afterward, “and @bleunightcluboc for such an incredible venue. I had so much fun and can’t wait to be back.” Two of the DJs on that bill are KEEPITIL applicants alongside her.
  • The convention circuit is where the anime side fully cashes in: she played Kawaii Kon in Hawaii — “I still can’t believe I played Knock2 at Kawaii Kon, Hawaii lol,” she wrote, and her YouTube channel carries the Kawaii Kon 2025 video — plus the Anime Expo LA After Party with Aniverses Events, Anikura (anime club) nights with Kohai Friends, and NijiFest’s Gay Rave.
  • The bass rooms keep calling too: Los Globos in Silver Lake for a full bass set with JUPTR Events, Bass City at the Helipad as special guest with DJ Jackrabbit (also her first b2b partner), the Bass Till Dawn OC afters in Huntington Beach, and TurtleTitans, where her b2b with Millusion was described as “pure chaos.”

More than a DJ: the Chlosphere

What separates Chlophonic from a long list of talented locals is that she is building a world, not just a set list. There is a merch line — her first booth at Chiikawa Rave, a collaboration collection with Takeover Studios, Chlophonic hats she designed “for fun” and then watched people want. There is fashion work: she modeled the AKIKO collection for MOKANO Studio. There is a vlog series on her YouTube channel (a DS Night Market vlog, Kawaii Kon footage, official “Chlophonic Visual” clips). And there is a stated philosophy, posted in March 2026 over event footage: “Chlophonic wasn’t built by me alone — it was built by the people around me.” She ended that post by asking fans to come say hi and take photos at her next gigs, “core memories” she says she wants to keep. For a booker, that translates directly: her crowd doesn’t just attend, it belongs.

What’s next

The immediate run is the strongest on her calendar. On Friday, October 23, 2026, she brings the bass to Bass Invaders at Dave & Buster’s in Ontario (4821 Mills Circle, 8 PM–2 AM), on a Party Pat / Bass Invaders / Marwave Productions bill — “who’s ready to break their necks with me?!” The next night, October 24, she is back on a five-DJ house bill for Brite Star Tavern’s haunted-house Halloween night. And in December she has been announced — in English and Japanese — as the fourth and final DJ for Team Future Funk at Takeover Fest 2, alongside DJ Zax, Mr. Chibes, and Unannannan. A DJ who can open a techno night in Orange County, headline a disco luau in DTLA, and hold a convention floor in Hawaii is not a niche act. She’s a utility player with a headline brand.

Why book Chlophonic

  • Four-genre range, verified set by set — dubstep/bass headline sets, house bills, a dedicated DnB set, a city pop set, and a techno debut, all documented on her own channels
  • A built-in audience — 11,000+ Instagram followers, 650+ posts of relentless self-promotion, and a named fan universe (“the Chlosphere”) that shows up in costume
  • Convention and anime-circuit credibility — Kawaii Kon Hawaii, Anime Expo after party, Anikura, NijiFest; very few DJs can credibly bill to both a rave crowd and a convention crowd
  • Professionally run — management contact, media team, merch operation, and a community-first reputation that promoters keep re-booking (Banana Bay, High Tide, The Compound, Kohai Friends all repeat)
  • A KEEPITIL connection already on the books — her first official techno set happened at KEEPITIL’s Keep It Techno night at BLEU Nightclub OC

The quick version

Chlophonic (Chloe Muangsaen) is a Thai-born, Santa Ana–representing DJ whose kawaii, anime-soaked brand wraps around a genuinely heavy multi-genre sound — dubstep, bass house, house, and DnB. A recurring face at Banana Bay, High Tide DTLA, and The Compound LA, a Kawaii Kon Hawaii alum, and the DJ who played her first official techno set at KEEPITIL’s Keep It Techno night at BLEU, she runs the act like a small company: management, media, merch, and a fan community she calls the Chlosphere. October 2026 alone puts her on five bills, capped by Bass Invaders in Ontario on October 23.
