# Store improvements shortlist for Muse (draft)

**Status: draft. No store changes were made and nothing was spent.** Based on a read-only look at the live Shopify store on 2026-11-05. Ranked by expected lift against effort, best first.

## What the data says (and does not say)
- **Catalogue:** 323 products. Print-on-demand merch is the bulk (flagship: Logo Tee $34, Hat $30, Crewneck $55, Hoodie $64). Also 18 collections, with 16 "Digital Marketing Packages", 18 "For Artists", 13 "Services" and 5 "Downloads".
- **Recent orders (newest 50 read):** since late February the only orders are **two digital orders** (Release Rollout Kit, one at $19.95 on Oct 28 and one free on Nov 1) and a $100 custom sale (Jun 7). The other 47 are small point-of-sale-style orders from Feb 21 and 28. **No merch orders appear in that window.**
- **Not read:** traffic, sessions, checkout starts and conversion rate (not exposed read-only here), so I cannot say whether the gap is traffic or conversion. Items 1 and 2 below fix that.
- Because merch demand looks very low right now, **#68's Tee+Hat bundle and free-shipping bar are low-lift until traffic to /shop exists.** Treat them as later items.

## Ranked shortlist

| # | Improvement | Lift | Effort | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Look at the funnel first:** pull Shopify Analytics (sessions to /shop, product views, add-to-cart, checkout starts) for the last 90 days | Enables all the rest | 15 minutes, no change | Tells us whether the problem is no visitors or visitors who don't buy. |
| 2 | **Lead /shop with the digital products** (Release Rollout Kit and the For Artists items) above the merch | Medium to high | Low (page order and a banner) | The only recent sales are digital. Put the thing that sells where visitors land. |
| 3 | **Turn the free kit into a lead magnet:** ask for email consent on the free Release Rollout Kit and send one follow-up pointing to the paid kit | Medium | Medium | One free order (Nov 1) already shows demand. Needs email consent wording and a sending tool. |
| 4 | **Collections cleanup:** "Tees" (75) and "T-Shirts & Tees" (68) overlap and "All Merch" lists 304; lead with the 6-item "KEEPITIL" collection | Medium | Low | A short, clear first collection beats a wall of 300 items. Hide or retitle the duplicates. |
| 5 | **Flagship product-page copy** for the 6 KEEPITIL items: fit, fabric, sizing, delivery estimate, returns, one real photo | Medium | Low to medium | Much of the catalogue still uses generic print-on-demand description text. The flagship items should sound like KEEPITIL. |
| 6 | **Shipping messaging:** say "Merch ships at a flat $6.69" and the real delivery window on product pages and the cart (no price change) | Low to medium | Low | Cheap clarity at the decision point. Check the real delivery estimate with the supplier first. |
| 7 | **Tee + Hat bundle at $59** (#68), then the $75 free-shipping bar once shipping cost is known | Low until traffic exists | Medium | Margins hold (bundle is 55.4% before fees). Do after items 1 to 5. |

Items 1, 2 and 4: about an hour of work between them, all reversible, and none touches prices.
