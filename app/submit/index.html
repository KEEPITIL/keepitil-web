For developers

List your app on KEEPITIL

The KEEPITIL store shows apps and games that have their own page on keepitil.com. If you are building something you would like listed, tell us about it by email and the KEEPITIL team will review it.

How it works

  1. Email us the details below.One email per app or game is easiest for us to review.
  2. The KEEPITIL team reviews it.We look at what the app does, where it runs, and how it treats people's data.
  3. We reply by email.If it is a fit, we will work with you on its page and listing.
Email info@keepitil.com

This page does not send anything itself. The button opens your own email app.

What to include

  • App or game name, and a one-line subtitle
  • Whether it is an app or a game, and what it does in a few sentences
  • Where it runs today (web link, App Store, or both)
  • A square icon (512×512 PNG) and one or two screenshots
  • Your privacy policy link and a support contact
  • Who it is for, including whether it is made for children

Apps made for children

Kids' apps are listed with no tracking and no links out of the app's own page. Please tell us up front if your app is made for children.
