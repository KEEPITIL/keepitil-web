KMOB: Endless War is a KEEPITIL game you can play right now, in your browser, for free. No App Store download is required.

You command a tank, build a six-class army, and see how long you can hold the line. Every wave is a chance to get stronger — or lose everything. There is no final wave. The fight gets tougher as your army grows.

More than a tower defense game

KMOB is an endless strategy battle built for quick decisions and big comebacks. Your army is not decoration. How you build it, protect it, and spend your gold decides whether you make it through the next wave.

  • Plan your army. Build a War Plan across six soldier classes, inside a 300-point capacity. You can change that plan during battle as the enemy changes tactics.
  • Command the tank. Slide to line up your cannon, switch between automatic and manual deployment, and decide when your army attacks or holds.
  • Earn the next advantage. Spend battle gold on soldiers and upgrades. Defeat bosses to earn rewards and choose lasting bonuses.
  • Use four elemental powers. Fire burns, Ice slows, Poison weakens healing, and Lightning jumps between enemies.
  • See the bigger battle. Zoom out up to 3×, or speed play up to 5×, when you are ready to move faster.

You start with 1,000 gold. A boss arrives every ten waves.

There is no perfect army — only the one you build

Balanced squads survive differently from heavy front lines. The six classes are:

  1. Looter — collects gold and supports the tank
  2. Archer — ranged support from behind your line
  3. Sword — your core frontline defenders
  4. Mizard — heals and keeps your army alive
  5. Elite — heavy-hitting armored specialists
  6. Giant — massive power, at a capacity cost

Try a new War Plan every run.

Why we are putting the web first

Play KMOB on a phone or a desktop browser. Add it to your home screen for a convenient icon — that is not the same as a native App Store download.

On the web, you get every tested, approved update first, automatically, and faster than an App Store release. We ship what you ask for. If you want a feature, a mode, or a fix, tell us — it shapes what we build next.

The current browser game is free to play. Optional purchases and future releases may add more choices later. KMOB has no ads and no tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share player data. You can play without an account.

What is still ahead

We are building KMOB as an evolving strategy world, not a one-time mini-game. Future directions being explored include competitive battles, a Crusade experience, and deeper ways to personalize your army. These are planned. They are not in today's browser game, and there is no confirmed date for KMOB 2.0.
